## Finds, continued
- **Deposit:** a round grey boulder, 3 in each Cave room; three bumps
  break it for a stone (D-311, D-312).
- **Pond:** still water in most woods rooms, 5 to 9 tiles; a thick-woods room where none fits has none (D-503, D-734).
- **Pool:** still water in a Cave room; not yet to step in (D-500).
- **Stone:** a small grey pebble from a deposit, or lying loose in the
  mountain rooms; it goes into the stash chest, the Stone Pick and the
  garden bed (D-312, D-324, D-480, D-562, D-761).
- **Copper vein:** a deposit's boulder flecked with copper, one in each
  Cave room from depth 3; four bumps with the Stone Pick break it for
  Copper Ore, the first ore (D-450, D-480).
- **Stone Pick:** the first pick, made at the workbench, held in the
  Tool slot, where it breaks copper (D-480, D-552); one waits at the
  miners' camp (D-770).
- **Mole Claw Pick:** made at the workbench from 1 Mole Claw, 2 Wood and
  2 Stone; held, it works as the Stone Pick, copper veins and rubble in 2 bumps (D-810).
- **Wooden Rod:** the first rod, made at the workbench, held in the
  Tool slot; fishing casts with it (D-608, D-609).
- **Bone Hook:** made at the workbench from 3 Rat Bone and 1 Spider Silk,
  fitted to the rod for good; the strike meter's zone half again as wide (D-807).
- **Pond Minnow:** the first fish, from any woods room's pond (D-734); poured for 15% oil, eaten for 1 heart, sold for 1 coin (D-608, D-610, D-611, D-615, D-616).
- **Blind Cavefish:** the cave pools' fish, pale and eyeless; poured for 20% oil, eaten for 1 heart, sold for 2 coins (D-618).
- **Float:** the line's red-and-white bob on the water while it waits; a bite puts a "!" over it (D-610).
- **Nibble:** the turn before a bite, the float shaking, "A nibble..." (D-613).
- **Strike meter:** the card a pulled bite opens, a needle swept across a bar; tap in the amber zone to land the fish (D-614).
- **Wood:** sticks, branches and trees all give it, for crafts and building;
  sticks lie in the woods in three looks (D-314, D-315).
- **Fallen branch:** one in each woods room, blocking a tile; two bumps
  break it for 2 Wood (D-316).
- **Stump:** what a chopped tree leaves; four bumps fell a tree for 3 Wood,
  and the stump grows back into it (D-317).
- **Grow back:** a picked thing returning after its time (D-306).
- **Lightkeeper's Locket:** biome 1's first relic, an open gold locket on
  a chain, a violet hollow with a flame inside, in a violet glow; one lies
  at depth 4 with a warden guarding it; set on the shelf for good, where
  a Normal fall brings home the bag's stack worth most to the peddler
  (D-689, D-691, D-692, D-693, D-694).
- **Warden's Fist:** the warden's special find, a fist of old stone with
  a gold band at the wrist and a violet stone; a beaten warden always
  leaves it. Held, it hits for 2 and knocks the creature back a tile, a
  wall or another creature hurting it; the first of the hammer kind
  (D-636, D-885, D-888).

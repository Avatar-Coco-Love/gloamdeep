## From "how will it be special?" (2026-11-21, D-1020)
The owner asked what makes Gloamdeep unique and how far it can go.
These ideas, Claude's from that talk, the owner wants kept.
- **Light decides the boss fights**: a signature for every chapter's
  boss, built on light, darkness, oil and the lantern's switch. Only one
  of the 20 games in the boss study does it (Darkest Dungeon's Shambler,
  `RESEARCH-GAMES.md`, way 5), so it is open ground. The Lampeater is a
  start. Builds on "Bosses in phases that test what you know"
  (`RESEARCH-2.md`).
- **Your lights, a map of your journey**: lit braziers and torches stay
  lit (D-015), so the world you have explored slowly fills with your own
  light, and progress is something you see. Builds on "Lit torches stay
  lit" (`RESEARCH-2.md`, D-658).
- **The surface answers the deep**: the gloam rises and lights fail on
  the surface (`LORE.md`, "The call"); each win below, such as a boss
  beaten, could light a lamp at home again or push the gloam back a
  little there, so the surface shows how far the hero has come.
- **Other players' light**, asynchronous (shared without playing at the
  same time): a lantern another player left lit, or a note on a
  waystone. Rare in phone RPGs. It would mean sending something off the
  device, against the plan so far (play data kept on the device, D-993;
  nothing sent, roadmap 7.11), so it waits for its own question.
- **Light-routing puzzles as a signature**: mirrors and lenses bending
  light onto crystals (the owner's terminal idea in `RESEARCH.md`,
  "Stories"), grown into a family of puzzles in the world. See
  `PUZZLES*.md` before proposing one.
- **The bait is the player's own pull**: the gloam spreads tales of
  treasure because people carry light down (`LORE.md`); the urge to go
  one floor deeper for better loot is the same pull. A thought for
  writing lore lines, Maren's pages and the fall card, not a mechanic.

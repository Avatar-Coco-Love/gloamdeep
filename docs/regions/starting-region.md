# The starting region

Rule sheet for the first region (step 42, D-182 to D-185). Uses the
template in `WORLD.md`. Where this sheet and a decision disagree, the
decision wins.

## Name and place
- **Name:** Hearthwood (D-348). "Gloamwood" stays free for a darker
  woods later.
- **Place:** the surface, where every journey starts (D-022).
- **Biome:** woods and mountains (D-028).

## Grid (D-182)
3 × 3 cells. All 9 are used, every seed, in this region (other regions
may leave cells empty).

```
 N   [mountain] [mountain] [mountain]
     [  woods ] [  HOME  ] [  woods ]
 S   [  woods ] [  woods ] [  woods ]
```

## Kinds of room (D-183)
- The north row (3 cells): mountain rooms, every seed.
- The other 6 cells: woods rooms.
- How big each kind is and how its shape is made: Q-038.

## Fixed promises, every seed
- **Home base** in the centre cell (D-182). The journey starts here and
  you wake here after dying (D-020).
- **The first cave's entrance** in one of the four side cells next to
  home: north, east, south or west, picked by the seed (D-184). If north,
  it is a mountain room. Until the grid is built, the mouth sits on that
  edge of the one woods room (D-211).

## Caves (D-184, D-185)
- One cave entrance for now: the first cave. The sheet leaves room for
  more caves later.
- **The first cave** is today's game (D-175): the two crypt rooms, with
  a third south of the first through a doorway (D-197), then generated
  depths 3 to 5. Depth 5 is its bottom, 5 levels in all.
- A portal at the bottom leads to the endless depths, renewed on each
  visit (D-019, D-032). The earth warrens and the dungeon stay below depth 5 for
  now; the bottom and its portal wait for the portal step (D-354).
- The first cave gets its own rule sheet when it is built.

## NPC houses (D-184)
None yet. A slot is kept for later; who lives where is decided when NPCs
exist.

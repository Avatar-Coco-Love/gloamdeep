## Finds
- **Oilcap:** an amber mushroom, the first resource, one in every
  generated room; 3 make an oil flask (D-296, D-299).
- **Heartcap:** a bright red mushroom in about half the rooms; Eat, in the
  bag, heals 1 heart (D-303, D-308); 3 make a healing potion (D-309).
- **Gloamberry:** a dark blue berry picked from a gloamberry bush in
  the woods, 2 a bump; Eat, in the bag, for 1 heart (D-531); 3 make a
  Gloamberry Seed (D-564).
- **Gloamberry Seed:** a brown pip with a tiny green shoot, made in the
  bag from 3 Gloamberries; planted in a garden bed (D-564, D-566).
  Not a world's **Seed**.
- **Gloamberry bush:** a low round bush, one in each woods room; picked,
  it stands **bare** and fills again 300 turns later (D-530, D-531).
- **Duskstar:** the first flower, five violet petals round a yellow
  heart; 2 in each woods room; at the mortar, 2 with 1 Wisp Glass make the
  Glow Draught, 1 with 2 Lurker Claw the Shade Draught; picked, it
  comes back a day later on another grass tile of the room (D-763).
- **Healing Potion:** a red bottle made from 3 heartcaps; Drink, in the
  bag, heals 4 hearts (D-309).
- **Mortar and Pestle:** a grey stone bowl on a wooden stump, made in
  the bag from 4 Stone and 1 Wood, set down on the surface; bumped, its
  card, MORTAR, lists what it makes: the Glow Draught, Slowburn, the
  Mending Draught, the Ward Draught, the Spitproof Draught and the Shade
  Draught (D-786, D-789, D-792, D-794, D-795, D-802, D-806).
- **Glow Draught:** a gold bottle made at the mortar from 2 Duskstar and
  1 Wisp Glass; Drink, any time: the lantern reaches 1 tile farther for
  60 turns, its gold bottle mark under the hearts, its time left on its
  rim (D-786, D-791, D-847).
- **Slowburn:** a bottle dark as lamp oil made at the mortar from 2 Tick
  Shell and 1 Oilcap; Drink, any time: the lantern burns slower for 60
  turns, a full tank lasting 70, its amber bottle mark under the hearts,
  its time on its rim (D-792).
- **Mending Draught:** a moss green bottle made at the mortar from 2
  Mimic Leaf and 1 Healing Herb; Drink, any time: a heart every 15 turns for 60
  turns, 4 in all, its green bottle mark under the hearts, its time on
  its rim (D-794).
- **Ward Draught:** a pale green-white bottle made at the mortar from 2
  Centipede Leg and 1 Healing Herb; Drink, any time: ends poisoned, and for 60
  turns no bite poisons, its pale bottle mark under the hearts, its
  time on its rim (D-795).
- **Spitproof Draught:** a dusty rose bottle made at the mortar from 2
  Grub Skin and 1 Healing Herb; Drink, any time: for 60 turns a spit grub's
  glob splashes off and hurts nothing, its rose bottle mark under the
  hearts, its time on its rim (D-802).
- **Shade Draught:** a dusky violet bottle made at the mortar from 2
  Lurker Claw and 1 Duskstar; Drink, any time: for 60 turns creatures
  notice the hero only within 2 steps, its violet bottle mark under the
  hearts, its time on its rim (D-806).
- **Antidote:** a tall thin green vial, made in the bag from 1 Snapcap Gill and 1 herb;
  Drink, in the bag, ends poisoned and heals nothing (D-655).
- **Torch (set down):** made from 1 Moth Dust and 1 Wood, set down on a
  floor tile anywhere; it burns 100 turns, then goes out (D-478).
- **Glow Lamp:** made in the bag from 1 Glow Gland and 1 Wood, set down
  as a torch is; its steady green light lasts 300 turns (D-808).
- **Campfire (in the bag):** made in the bag from 3 Stone and 2 Wood,
  one carried, drawn as cold ashes; set down on the surface as cold
  ashes, lit with oil (D-850, D-851).
- **Eat, Drink:** the bag's buttons for a heartcap and a potion (D-308, D-309), Drink an antidote's too (D-655); a minnow's Eat sits left of its Pour (D-615).
- **Creature materials:** what a beaten creature leaves: Rat Tail, Moth
  Dust, Stinger, Mimic Glass, Gloam Wisp; rare ones Venom Sac, Mimic
  Heart, Gloam Eye (D-453, D-454). All eight are in the game (D-474
  to D-476). The cave spider's Spider Silk is the ninth (D-535);
  the bat's Bat Wing and rare Bat Fang the tenth and eleventh (D-537);
  the mole's Mole Fur and rare Mole Claw, for the Mole Claw Pick (D-810), the twelfth and thirteenth (D-541);
  the snapcap's Snapcap Gill the fourteenth (D-543); the herb mimic's Mimic
  Leaf the fifteenth (D-549); the bone rat's Rat Bone, for the Bone Hook (D-807), the sixteenth (D-551);
  the oil tick's Tick Shell and rare Oil Bladder the seventeenth and eighteenth (D-606);
  the lure wisp's Wisp Glass, sold for 2, the nineteenth (D-649); the centipede's
  Centipede Leg the twentieth, and its rare one a Venom Sac, as the scorpion's (D-654);
  the spit grub's Grub Skin and rare Glow Gland the twenty-first and twenty-second (D-688);
  the bunny's Bunny Fur, for the Fur Bedroll (D-803), the twenty-third (D-784);
  the skulker crab's Crab Shell, the twenty-fourth (D-796);
  the rim lurker's Lurker Claw, the twenty-fifth (D-797).
- **Bat Guano:** a pile of bat droppings, one beside each bat as a room
  is made; it grows back, and is kept for the garden (D-539, D-540).
- **Tone:** a mushroom's own slight shift of colour (D-304, D-305).
- **Deposit to the Locket:** stone, ore, picks, fishing, wood and the
  first relic moved to `GLOSSARY-FINDS-2.md` word for word at 0.3.329
  (D-884).

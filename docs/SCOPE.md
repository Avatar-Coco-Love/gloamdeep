# Scope: where the game's things are going

The full plan for items, resources, collectables, harvestables,
craftables, experience and skills, so content is added toward a known
shape, not one item at a time blindly (D-413). It is drawn up in parts,
each asked as multiple choice before it is written. Only what a decision
settles is here; examples marked *(e.g.)* are not decided, and no name or
number below is final until its own step. Read this before proposing new
content, with `CONTENT.md` for what exists today.

## Part 1: the frame (settled)

### Size and needs (D-414)
- **About 100 to 150 things in all**, counting every resource, creature
  material, thing made, piece of gear and collectable (11 today). Each
  of the four biomes brings roughly 25 to 35 new ones, built one biome
  at a time.
- **No hunger.** Oil stays the only clock (pillar 1). Food is a bonus:
  it heals or gives a short boost *(e.g. see farther, hit harder, burn
  oil slower)*, worth cooking but never a chore.
- **Shared basics, and each biome's own.** Wood, stone and oil turn up
  everywhere; each biome adds a signature set, and deeper sets make
  better things, so every descent shows something new (pillar 2).
  *(e.g. copper in the mines, glowcaps in the fungal caverns, sunken
  brass in the ruins.)*
- **Gear and crafting lead** the four ways of getting stronger (D-038):
  most power comes from what is made and found; levels and embers give a
  steady, smaller climb; lore gives small, rare boosts. This is the
  owner's loop: deeper → find → craft and grow → deeper.

### Making and loot (D-415)
Seven craft families; every recipe belongs to one:

| Family | What it makes |
| --- | --- |
| Lampcraft | the signature craft: oil, wicks, lenses, torches, flares, lantern upgrades |
| Cooking and alchemy | food that heals and boosts; potions, salves, antidotes (today's potion) |
| Smithing and tools | metal from ore: weapons with attack shapes (D-027), picks, axes, a rod |
| Building | home pieces and furniture (today's workbench and chest) |
| Leatherwork | armour and bigger bags from hides and cloth |
| Electronic | from old machines in the mines: wire, cells, power (the batteries idea) |
| Arcane | from the ruins and rifts: runes, gloam crystals |

- **Electronic and arcane are found below, rebuilt at home** (the
  owner's addition): pieces are found deep down, and each track's
  station is then built at home, a late discovery of its own (pillar 3).
- **Fixed items, rare finer versions.** A thing is always the same thing,
  easy to learn. Portal depths, which promise rarer loot (D-019), may
  give a rare finer version with one small extra *(e.g. "fine",
  "gloam-touched")*.
- **No wear.** What is made is kept; oil, food and potions are what get
  used up.
- **Tools open tougher materials,** as in Stardew Valley or Terraria:
  each better tool opens new materials, and new places.

### Growth and skills (D-419, supersedes D-416)
Two ways, each with its own job:
- **Embers buy a Growth tree:** steady upgrades, growing from today's
  hearts *(e.g. a slower oil burn, a stronger bite, more slots)*.
- **Big abilities are found,** as in Hollow Knight: from shrines, relics
  or bosses deep down, each opening new paths *(e.g. the double jump,
  a dash, the elemental powers)*.
- Learning by doing and skills taught by NPCs are left out for now, to
  keep growth simple; either may come back by a new decision. NPCs may
  still teach or sell recipes (D-418).

### Creature drops (D-417, narrows Q-061)
- **Its own common material, and some have a rare one,** each with its
  own chance from the seed (D-372). Hunting one creature for one recipe
  is worth it.
- **What they are for:** gear and armour, potions and lampcraft, bestiary
  pages and trophies at home, and selling to vendors.

### Money, the bag and recipes (D-418)
- **Two currencies.** Trade coins come from selling to vendors and buy
  useful things *(e.g. recipes, seeds, rare materials, tools)*. The
  look currency (Q-055) comes only from deeds, records and achievements,
  and buys only looks, so looking good never costs strength (D-280).
- **The bag has slots that grow by crafting:** leatherwork makes pouches
  and bigger bags *(e.g. a pouch only for herbs or ore)*. A full bag is
  a reason to go home. No weight.
- **Recipes are learned three ways:** a recipe shows once the hero has
  held its parts; special ones come from notes, machines and shrines
  found deep down; and NPCs teach or sell some.

## Part 2: raw things (settled)
- **Ore (D-450):** one new metal per biome for now, each a step better
  than the one above; more may come later. *(e.g. copper in the mines.)*
- **Tools open things (D-450):** better picks open the next ore; stone
  stays breakable by hand. Better axes open the deeper woods. Tools open
  places too *(e.g. a pick through a cracked wall, a rod across water)*.
- **Plants (D-451):** each biome adds 2 or 3, one an oil plant like the
  oilcap, deeper ones giving more oil.
- **Wood and stone (D-451):** plain wood up top, one wood of each deeper
  biome's own. Stone stays plain; a biome stone may come later.
- **Growing back (D-451):** today's speeds (D-306): plants 300 turns,
  trees and deposits 900, rarer things slower.
- **Fishing (D-452):** any water, surface and below, with the right tools.
- **Farming (D-452):** a garden at home; seeds grow while the hero is
  below. Gardening away from home may come later.

## Part 3: creature drops (settled)
- **Chances (D-453):** the common material about 1 in 2; a rare one,
  about 1 in 10, only for creatures from depth 3 down. Fixed for now;
  portal depths or a charm may raise them later.
- **Picking up (D-453):** a drop lies where the creature fell; step on
  it to take it. A full bag leaves it there.
- **Today's five (D-454):** Rat Tail; Moth Dust; Stinger, rare Venom
  Sac; Mimic Glass, rare Mimic Heart; Gloam Wisp, rare Gloam Eye. Each
  leans to what fits its creature; the rare ones to finer gear.

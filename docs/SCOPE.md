# Scope: where the game's things are going

The full plan for items, resources, collectables, harvestables,
craftables, experience and skills, so content is added toward a known
shape, not one item at a time blindly (D-413). It is drawn up in parts,
each asked as multiple choice before it is written. Only what a decision
settles is here; examples marked *(e.g.)* are not decided, and no name or
number below is final until its own step. Read this, and part 1 in
`SCOPE-FRAME.md`, before proposing new content, with `CONTENT.md` for
what exists today.

## Part 2: raw things (settled)
- **Ore (D-450):** one new metal per biome for now, each a step better
  than the one above; more may come later. *(e.g. copper in the mines.)*
- **Tools open things (D-450):** better picks open the next ore; stone
  stays breakable by hand. Better axes open the deeper woods. Tools open
  places too *(e.g. a pick through a cracked wall, a rod across water)*.
- **Plants (D-451):** each biome adds 2 or 3, one an oil plant like the
  oilcap, deeper ones giving more oil.
- **Wood and stone (D-451):** plain wood up top, one wood of each deeper
  biome's own. Stone stays plain; a biome stone may come later.
- **Growing back (D-451):** today's speeds (D-306): plants 300 turns,
  trees and deposits 900, rarer things slower.
- **Fishing (D-452):** any water, surface and below, with the right tools.
- **Farming (D-452):** a garden at home; seeds grow while the hero is
  below. Gardening away from home may come later.

## Part 3: creature drops (settled)
- **Chances (D-453):** the common material about 1 in 2; a rare one,
  about 1 in 10, only for creatures from depth 3 down. Fixed for now;
  portal depths or a charm may raise them later.
- **Picking up (D-453):** a drop lies where the creature fell; step on
  it to take it. A full bag leaves it there.
- **Today's five (D-454):** Rat Tail; Moth Dust; Stinger, rare Venom
  Sac; Mimic Glass, rare Mimic Heart; Gloam Wisp, rare Gloam Eye. Each
  leans to what fits its creature; the rare ones to finer gear.

## Part 4: making (settled)
- **Where (D-455):** small things *(e.g. flasks, potions, torches)* in
  the bag, anywhere; building, tools and leatherwork at the workbench.
  Later each bigger family gets a station of its own, built at home: a
  forge (smithing), a kitchen fire (cooking), a power bench (electronic),
  an altar (arcane). Stations stand only on the surface.
- **The ladder (D-456):** about 8 to 12 new things to make in each
  biome. Most rungs open by their materials (D-418) and better tools
  (D-450); a few special ones, about 1 or 2 a biome, plus the electronic
  and arcane stations, are found below. Stations don't improve for now.

## Part 5: gear (settled)
- **Weapons (D-457):** about 4 or 5 kinds, each with its own attack
  shape *(e.g. knife 1 tile, spear 2 in a line, axe a sweep of 3, a
  sling at range)*. Each biome's metal makes a better one of each kind:
  the shape stays, the hits grow. One in hand; others in the bag,
  swapped there, a swap taking a turn.
- **Worn (D-458):** one armour piece (leatherwork) and two charms, each
  with one small effect *(e.g. better drop odds, a slower burn)*.
- **The lantern (D-459):** a few part slots *(e.g. tank, wick, glass)*,
  many parts for each, one per slot, swapped from the bag anywhere;
  made at the workbench. Oil alone still sets the reach (D-014).
- **Other light (D-459):** torches, made in the bag and set down on a
  tile. Flares, glowing gear and machine lights are left out for now.
- **Fishing (D-460):** one rod, better by each biome's metal; bait may
  come later.

## Part 6: growth (settled)
- **Levels (D-461):** today's curve, each level 10 more experience than
  the last, 1 ember each, no cap.
- **The Growth tree (D-462):** three branches: Light *(e.g. slower
  burn)*, Body (hearts, *e.g.* bite, guard) and Hands *(e.g. bag slots,
  faster gathering, drop odds)*. Gathering skills are Hands upgrades.
  Each branch's next row opens on reaching the next biome; 1 ember each.
- **Found abilities (D-463):** one per biome, at a shrine or from a boss
  near its bottom, each opening paths in places already seen *(e.g. a
  leap over a 1-tile gap)*.

## Part 7: collectables (settled)
- **Lore (D-464):** notes, carvings and murals found below fill a Lore
  tab in the Journal, in sets *(e.g. 5 pages of one story)*; a finished
  set gives one small lasting boost.
- **Relics (D-465):** rare finds, about 1 or 2 a biome, set on a shelf
  at home, each giving one small lasting boost while there.
- **Trophies (D-466):** one per creature, made at the workbench from its
  materials, hung at home; it completes its Bestiary page. No strength.
- **Achievements (D-467):** 3 tiers a chosen record, each paying the
  look currency, shown in a Feats tab. Looks stay bought with that
  currency only (D-280, D-418).

## Part 8: home and trade (settled)
- **Home (D-469):** grows by the pieces built there with the Building
  craft *(e.g. a bed, shelves, the stations)*; no home level. Stations
  still don't improve (D-456).
- **Rest (D-469):** sleeping at home gives a short boost for the next
  trip *(e.g. a little extra oil or a heart)*.
- **People (D-470):** most are rescued below and move home, each giving
  it a use *(e.g. a vendor, a teacher)*. Later, some stay where they are
  met *(e.g. the cult in a lit place below)*, and wanderers visit
  *(e.g. a wandering merchant)*.
- **The first vendor (D-471):** a rescued person who sets up at home,
  buying creature materials and spare finds, selling *(e.g.)* seeds,
  recipes and basics. More vendors come with more rescues.
- **Trade coins (D-472):** come only from selling to vendors, never
  dropped or found, so finds stay the reward. The look currency stays
  deeds only (D-418, Q-055).

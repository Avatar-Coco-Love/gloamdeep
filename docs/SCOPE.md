# Scope: where the game's things are going

The full plan for items, resources, collectables, harvestables,
craftables, experience and skills, so content is added toward a known
shape, not one item at a time blindly (D-413). It is drawn up in parts,
each asked as multiple choice before it is written. Only what a decision
settles is here; examples marked *(e.g.)* are not decided, and no name or
number below is final until its own step. Read this, and part 1 in
`SCOPE-FRAME.md`, before proposing new content, with `CONTENT.md` for
what exists today.

## Part 2: raw things (settled)
- **Ore (D-450):** one new metal per biome for now, each a step better
  than the one above; more may come later. *(e.g. copper in the mines.)*
- **Tools open things (D-450):** better picks open the next ore; stone
  stays breakable by hand. Better axes open the deeper woods. Tools open
  places too *(e.g. a pick through a cracked wall, a rod across water)*.
- **Plants (D-451):** each biome adds 2 or 3, one an oil plant like the
  oilcap, deeper ones giving more oil.
- **Wood and stone (D-451):** plain wood up top, one wood of each deeper
  biome's own. Stone stays plain; a biome stone may come later.
- **Growing back (D-451):** today's speeds (D-306): plants 300 turns,
  trees and deposits 900, rarer things slower.
- **Fishing (D-452):** any water, surface and below, with the right tools.
- **Farming (D-452):** a garden at home; seeds grow while the hero is
  below. Gardening away from home may come later.

## Part 3: creature drops (settled)
- **Chances (D-453):** the common material about 1 in 2; a rare one,
  about 1 in 10, only for creatures from depth 3 down. Fixed for now;
  portal depths or a charm may raise them later.
- **Picking up (D-453):** a drop lies where the creature fell; step on
  it to take it. A full bag leaves it there.
- **Today's five (D-454):** Rat Tail; Moth Dust; Stinger, rare Venom
  Sac; Mimic Glass, rare Mimic Heart; Gloam Wisp, rare Gloam Eye. Each
  leans to what fits its creature; the rare ones to finer gear.

## Part 4: making (settled)
- **Where (D-455):** small things *(e.g. flasks, potions, torches)* in
  the bag, anywhere; building, tools and leatherwork at the workbench.
  Later each bigger family gets a station of its own, built at home: a
  forge (smithing), a kitchen fire (cooking), a power bench (electronic),
  an altar (arcane). Stations stand only on the surface.
- **The ladder (D-456):** about 8 to 12 new things to make in each
  biome. Most rungs open by their materials (D-418) and better tools
  (D-450); a few special ones, about 1 or 2 a biome, plus the electronic
  and arcane stations, are found below. Stations don't improve for now.

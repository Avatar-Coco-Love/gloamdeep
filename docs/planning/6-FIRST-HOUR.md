# Area 6: The first hour, on a phone
This area looked at how a new player learns the game, how the controls and menus feel under a thumb, and how readable everything is on a small screen.

## Now
- **6.01 Teach sleep and the map** · builds on D-705 · The teaching plan left sleep and the map "to check in a playtest". Watch a new player, then add a teaching goal for any that is not found alone. One goal a step.
- **6.02 Teach Leap's first use** · builds on D-705, D-989 · Leap is in How to play, but a new hero meets a gap with no push. A goal line, "Jump a gap", would appear the first time a Leap gap is seen once Leap is earned.
- **6.03 Teach the Lampeater fight** · builds on D-705, D-630 · The boss changes by phase and is hurt only when lit. A short Bestiary line or goal after first meeting, so the arena teaches rather than punishes.
- **6.04 Phone playtest of the first hour** · builds on D-705, D-975 · One timed playtest on a real phone with a fresh profile, noting where the player stalls or asks "what now?". Findings go to the owner as small steps.
- **6.05 Gentler first mole** · builds on D-975 · H-71 found the first-day mole takes 3 of 5 hearts from a new hero. The owner picks: a gentler mole, none in the start room on day 1, or as is.
- **6.06 Text line never covers the hero** · builds on D-975 · H-75 found the text line can cover the hero low in a room. Move the line up, or draw the hero on top, so the thing a new player must see stays seen.
- **6.07 Smallest-view check for cards** · builds on D-513 · B-013 saw DECONSTRUCT under the bag's slots on the smallest view. Run every card on that size once and fix any button that a slot overlaps.

## Next
- **6.08 Teaching goal for each new system** · builds on D-705, D-640 · Each chapter adds a system (forge, kitchen fire, Swim, Flare). Give each one small goal line and a Quests entry the first time it matters, so the habit carries on.
- **6.09 A "what now?" hint after a long wait** · new: needs the owner's OK · If a player stands idle or loops a floor, the spark could say one short line pointing to the goal. Off in Settings, never a quest marker on the map.
- **6.10 Bigger text option** · new: needs the owner's OK · A SCREEN tab choice for larger words on cards and the text line, for small phones and tired eyes. Cards would scroll rather than shrink.
- **6.11 Colour-blind friendly marks** · new: needs the owner's OK · Check that danger, poison, lit and dark do not rest on colour alone, and add a shape or mark where they do. Builds on the Attack marks setting.

## Toward publishing
- **6.12 Phone-size and safe-area pass** · builds on D-590, D-513 · Store needs safe areas, the Android back button and 44 px taps. Test notches and rotation on a few real phone sizes and fix anything cut off.
- **6.13 Faster first load** · builds on D-975, D-430 · H-82: 299 files load before the title. Measure on a phone, then load cards and dev tools when first used, so the first screen appears quickly.
- **6.14 Start-up screen and the first 60 seconds** · builds on Q-063, D-628 · Decide the maker's name screen, then trim the path from icon tap to the first step in the woods. Aim for one tap past the opening card.
- **6.15 Testers' first-hour questions** · builds on D-590 · The 12 testers for 14 days could get a three-question note on day 1: where they got stuck, what they did not understand, what they enjoyed. Feeds the teaching goals.

## Someday
- **6.16 Left-hand and thumb-reach layout** · wild · new: needs the owner's OK · Let the arrow pad, glass panel and buttons mirror or move, so the game suits any hand and any size of phone, with the world still shown the same.
- **6.17 A skippable ten-minute guided start** · wild · builds on D-658 · The vertical-slice idea, used as teaching: a short scripted first descent with the spark that new players may play or skip, which also serves as the store trailer.

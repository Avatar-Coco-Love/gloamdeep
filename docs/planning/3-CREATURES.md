# Area 3: Creatures and bosses
This area looked at each chapter's creatures, one boss a chapter, and what building the Lampeater taught about making the next bosses.

## Now
- **3.01 The Grave Beetle** · builds on D-643, D-636 · The last planned chapter 1 kind, still unbuilt: a shelled beetle on the deeper floors whose shell turns blows aside, waiting for the hammer that cracks shells in chapter 2.
- **3.02 A boss field in the registry** · builds on D-983 · Before any second boss, give each boss a `boss` field in the creature list so the 5 files that look for the Lampeater by name read that instead. Nothing seen changes.
- **3.03 Tune the Lampeater for Lean heroes** · builds on Q-111, D-948, D-958 · Lean wins about 25-34% on the boss bench. Offer the owner one knob (its hearts, its bite, or how long a catch holds) and re-run the bench.
- **3.04 Lure wisps that show where it lurks** · builds on D-939, D-649 · D-939 said lure wisps show where the Lampeater waits; add one or two to the arena, drifting toward it so the dark gives a hint.
- **3.05 The Lampeater's material and trophy** · builds on Q-061, D-466, D-954 · A material it leaves when beaten, and its trophy made at the workbench and hung at home, finishing its Bestiary page.
- **3.06 Its story in three places** · builds on D-993, D-464 · A lore page on the old seal, its Bestiary page as it is now, and a rumour from the guide on depths 6 to 7, as D-993 approved for every boss.
- **3.07 The boss recipe** · builds on D-939, D-948, D-957, D-959 · Write what the Lampeater taught as a short checklist: one light rule, an arena that fights too, a change at set hearts, helpers, a test bench, a cutscene and a hearts bar.

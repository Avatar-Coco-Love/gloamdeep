# Area 4: Crafting and home
This area looked at the 7 craft families, the stations still to come, and how home is built and grows, from chapter 1 to 1.0.

## Now
- **4.01 Gloam Eye glass** · builds on D-799, D-641, D-459 · After the tank (step 27), the lantern's first glass from the rare Gloam Eye, showing creatures in disguise, so all three part slots exist in chapter 1.
- **4.02 Stinger Spear** · builds on D-799, D-641 · The Stinger finally gets its planned use: a spear made at the workbench that poisons what it hits. One recipe, one visible change.
- **4.03 Venom Oil at the mortar** · builds on D-799, D-785 · The mortar's fifth recipe, from the rare Venom Sac: oil put on the weapon in hand for a while, a first taste of the Venom element.
- **4.04 Cooking at a campfire** · builds on D-785, Q-088, Q-093 · The first simple food cooked at a lit campfire, the Gloamberry's waiting recipe as the first dish. Numbers asked at its step.
- **4.05 Herb Seed in the garden** · builds on D-642, D-562 · The Herb Seed, made in the bag from 3 Healing Herbs like the Gloamberry Seed, so the garden bed grows a second plant; an Oilcap Seed after, growing slowly (D-642).
- **4.06 Trophy hook and the first trophy** · builds on D-466, D-954, Q-061 · A home piece to hang trophies, and the Lampeater's trophy as the first one, finishing its Bestiary page. Looks and pride only.

## Next
- **4.07 The forge** · builds on D-455, D-636, D-638, D-641 · Smithing's own station at home, its first make a copper knife (a bone with the metal), then the other weapon kinds, one a step: same shapes, harder hits.
- **4.08 The kitchen fire** · builds on D-771, D-785 · Cooking's station at home, after campfire food: the same dishes and a few that need the home fire, from each chapter's plants.
- **4.09 The power bench, found then rebuilt** · builds on D-415, D-456 · An old bench found broken in the mines; its pieces carried home rebuild it as the electronic family's station.
- **4.10 The altar, found then rebuilt** · builds on D-415, D-455, D-456 · Arcane's station, found in the sunken ruins and rebuilt at home, the last station of the four.
- **4.11 The alchemy lab** · builds on D-785, Q-093 · The better alchemy station after the mortar, with stronger draughts from deeper finds; the mortar stays for the old recipes.
- **4.12 Found recipe pages** · builds on D-456 · The 1 or 2 special recipes a chapter, found below as notes in caches or vaults, the rarer and finer things, the rest opening by their materials.
- **4.13 Each chapter's plants at home** · builds on D-642, D-451 · Lampwort, glowcap and ember lily seeds, each made from 3 of the plant, grown in the garden bed, the deeper oil plants slower still.

## Toward publishing
- **4.14 A Recipes page in the Journal** · new: needs the owner's OK · One list of every recipe held so far, which station makes it and what it still needs, so a phone player does not walk round every station to check.
- **4.15 The spark points home** · builds on D-633, D-473 · The first time the bag holds enough for the workbench or a station, the guide says one short line, so the first hour finds crafting.

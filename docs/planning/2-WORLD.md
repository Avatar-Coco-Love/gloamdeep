# Area 2: The world
What is left to build of chapter 1's world, and what makes the mines, the fungal caverns and the sunken ruins each their own: rooms, ways between them, puzzles.

## Now
- **2.01 The place beyond, filled** · builds on D-969, D-970, Q-115 · The empty room past the Archway gets its own look, a few creatures and a cache, then grows to 2 to 4 rooms, one room a step.
- **2.02 Leap gaps below depth 8** · builds on D-968, D-647 · The gaps D-968 left for later: on the floors under depth 8, a gap in a dead end, so Leap is used again before chapter 2 opens.
- **2.03 The gloam tide vault** · builds on D-890, Q-105 · T1, the last of the first four puzzles: gloam creeping in a row a turn while you solve a vault. Placed in the warrens or the dungeon, one fixed layout first.
- **2.04 One set piece for chapter 1** · builds on D-889, D-019 · A hand-made landmark room the seed places once, say a Lightkeeper's lit hall on depth 6, so the way down holds a story beat as well as floors.

## Next
- **2.05 The mines' rule sheet** · builds on D-635, D-647, D-954 · Depths 9 to 16 get their own sheet, as Hearthwood has: grid sizes, room counts, the way, loops and where the boss room sits. Floors stop being copies of depth 8.
- **2.06 The mines' two kinds of place** · builds on D-640, D-028 · The seed picks each middle and lower floor as old workings (timbered tunnels, rails) or wild caves. Names are drafts; one kind built first, the other a later step.
- **2.07 Firedamp** · builds on D-889, D-014 · The mines bend pillar 1: gas pockets that blow up near a lit lantern, so you pass dark, or light it on purpose to blast rubble. One gas tile first, then its puzzles.
- **2.08 Minecarts and rails** · builds on D-889, D-171 · A cart on rails, pushed to roll and smash rubble or creatures; later a rail line as a way between rooms. The push from P1 grown into a mines puzzle.
- **2.09 The caverns light themselves** · builds on D-889, D-635 · Depths 17 to 24: glowing rooms where the lantern matters less, but spores burst when lit and mushrooms grow toward light into bridges. One self-lit room first.
- **2.10 The ruins' water level** · builds on D-889, D-171, Q-039 · Depths 25 to 32: valves that raise or lower the water across several rooms, opening some and closing others. Swim (D-636) later reaches the rooms only water leads to.
- **2.11 Lens beams to the First Light** · builds on D-889, D-028 · Great lens arrays in the ruins carry a beam room to room (L2), each turned lens lighting the way deeper. Ends at the First Light's door.
- **2.12 Old secrets for new abilities** · builds on D-640, D-636 · Heave boulders and Flare-only dark spots placed in chapter 1 from the start, marked on the map once seen, opened on a later trip back up.
- **2.13 A waystone look for each chapter** · builds on D-592, D-594 · The owner's wish: the stones on 12, 16, 20 and on take the chapter's look, a miners' post, a glowing stalk, a sunken plinth. Drafts, drawn in code.
- **2.14 The first portal** · builds on D-019, D-032 · One portal in chapter 1, then one a chapter: a twisted depth of that biome, remade each visit, rarer finds. W2's trial chain could be one kind later.

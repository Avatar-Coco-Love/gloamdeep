# Area 8: Outside critics
Two outside voices read the seven areas: a new player on a phone deciding in ten minutes whether to keep going, and a scope realist counting what one owner and Claude can build, a version a PR, before 1.0.

## Now
- **8.01 Finish chapter 1 in this order** · builds on D-991, D-954, Q-111 · Scope: reach 0.4.0 first: 1.01 tank, 3.03 Lampeater tuning, 6.05 first mole, 6.06 text line, 3.05 trophy. Hold 4.01 to 4.05, 2.04 and 5.03 until the way to depth 9 is open.
- **8.02 Merge the doubles** · builds on D-466, D-993, D-590, D-634 · Scope: one card each: 3.05 + 4.06 (trophy), 3.06 + 5.05 (Lampeater story), 6.07 + 6.12 + 7.07 (screens), 6.10 + 7.13 (big words), 1.15 + 6.15 + 7.14 (testers), 1.16 + 2.18 + 5.17 (no bottom, after launch).
- **8.03 The lantern in the first minute** · builds on D-013, D-014, D-628 · New player: check that within a minute of the opening card the player has seen the lantern light a room and the oil drop. If pillar 1 is not felt by then, fix that before any new content.
- **8.04 The first fall says nothing is lost** · builds on D-020, D-021, D-633 · New player: many will think a fall wipes the journey and quit. The first time, the spark says one short line that all is kept; 1.07's fall card shows what was found.
- **8.05 One phone playtest, many answers** · builds on D-705, D-975 · New player: fold 6.01, 6.02 and 6.04 into one timed fresh-profile playtest on a real phone; its notes decide which teaching goals to add, instead of guessing each one.
- **8.06 Count what a chapter costs** · builds on D-518, D-635 · Scope: at 0.4.0, count how many versions chapter 1 took. If chapters 2 to 4 need as many, 1.0 is far off; plan them to reuse 3.07's boss recipe and 2.05's rule sheet.

## Next
- **8.07 One rule-bender a chapter, to start** · builds on D-889, D-635 · Scope: each new place gets one rule that bends the lantern first: 2.07 firedamp OR 2.08 minecarts in the mines, not both; 2.09 alone in the caverns; 2.10 alone in the ruins. More only if time allows.
- **8.08 Ten kinds a chapter is a ceiling** · builds on D-643, Q-076 · Scope: aim for 6 new kinds a chapter plus carried-down ones (3.08), and one wanderer or rare named (3.13, 3.14, 5.09) per chapter; the other ~8 wanderers wait for after launch.
- **8.09 Portals after the four chapters** · builds on D-019, D-032 · Scope: 2.14 is a whole new system (remade depths, rarer finds). Build it once all four chapters stand, one biome's portal first, rather than one a chapter along the way.
- **8.10 Fewer stations, same crafting** · builds on D-455, D-456 · Scope: keep forge (4.07) and the found benches (4.09, 4.10); move 4.11 alchemy lab and 4.08 kitchen fire to Someday, letting the mortar and campfire (4.04) carry those recipes.
- **8.11 Music and the sound of the dark** · builds on Q-095, D-773 · New player: no area plans music, yet the dark feeling alive is pillar 1. Settle Q-095 early in chapter 2: a quiet drone a chapter, from the free pack, one track a step.
- **8.12 A goal waiting on every return** · builds on D-629, D-640, D-705 · New player: phone play comes in short visits. On opening a save, the Quests tab's top goal (5.07) shows on the text line once, so the player never starts with "what was I doing?".

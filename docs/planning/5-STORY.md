# Area 5: Story and lore
How the story of Underhearth, the First Light and Old Maren could unfold floor by floor as the hero goes down, from the first page to the end.

## Now
- **5.01 The Lore tab and Maren's first page** · builds on D-464, D-633 · The Journal gets its Lore tab, and the first of Maren's journal pages lies in the crypt. One small step: one page, its words the owner's pick.
- **5.02 Maren's pages for chapter 1** · builds on D-464, D-634 · About one page a floor down to depth 8 (30 to 40 over the 32 floors), together a set whose small lasting boost the owner picks. The pages tell the hero was there.
- **5.03 Maren's empty grave** · builds on D-633 · A grave in the crypt the Lightkeepers dug for Maren. Bumped, a short line that hurts before the hero knows why; the line changes once a few pages are found.
- **5.04 The spark freed** · builds on D-633 · The guide, a small warm glow beside the hero, freed from a trap early in chapter 1. It never lights rooms; for now it only follows and glows.
- **5.05 The Lampeater's history** · builds on Q-083, D-993 · Its three pieces: a lore page of its past, its Bestiary page of what it is now, and a guide's rumour of where it waits. Words drafted for the owner.
- **5.06 The start and the story, lined up** · builds on D-633, D-735, D-628 · LORE has the hearth pull the hero home to the crypt, but a journey now begins in the woods. A line or two to join them, the owner choosing which to change.
- **5.07 The story goal** · builds on D-629, D-705 · After the teaching goals, a first main story goal in the Quests tab, such as "Follow the call down", leading to depth 8.

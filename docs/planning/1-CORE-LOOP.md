# Area 1: Core loop and progression
How the lantern's parts, levels, embers and the "deeper, find, craft, deeper" loop feel across the whole journey, and how hard each chapter should be.

## Now
- **1.01 The first tank, drafted** · builds on D-991, D-641, D-459 · For step 27: two or three drafts of chapter 1's tank part (what it is made from, how much more oil it holds), with mock-ups, for the owner to pick from.
- **1.02 The lantern's three slots, shown** · builds on D-459, D-483 · Where a part is fitted, show the lantern's wick, tank and glass slots, empty ones dim, so the hero can see what is still out there to find.
- **1.03 A chapter 1 pacing bot** · builds on D-948, Q-111 · Grow the boss test bench into a dev tool that plays from the surface to depth 8 and counts turns, flasks burnt and found, LV and falls, one line per run.
- **1.04 An oil budget a floor** · builds on D-014, D-414, D-642 · Write down, as a draft, how many flasks a floor of chapter 1 burns against how many it gives, so oil stays a worry but rarely a wall; the bot in 1.03 checks it.
- **1.05 The hero at the Lampeater's door** · builds on D-461, D-639, Q-111 · Set a draft target for the LV, hearts and gear a hero has on reaching depth 8, then tune the Lampeater's hearts against Middling, not Lean.
- **1.06 Always an ember's worth to buy** · builds on D-461, D-462, D-814, D-896 · Count embers earned by depth 8 against open Growth places, so the hero never has nothing to buy and never sits on a pile unspent.
- **1.07 The fall card** · builds on D-658, D-020 · On a fall, a small card: how deep, what got you, what you found this trip; it feeds "how far can I get?" and costs no progress.

## Next
- **1.08 One rhythm for every chapter** · builds on D-635, D-641, D-638, D-463, D-462 · A draft beat sheet for 8 floors: Growth row opens on arrival, new metal early, a lantern part in the middle, a found ability from the boss, so something new turns up every two floors.
- **1.09 The tank and wick ladder** · builds on D-641, D-991 · One draft table for chapters 1 to 4: each tank and wick a plain step better than the last, so the owner sets all four rungs at once and each chapter only builds its own.
- **1.10 Glass as the trip's choice** · builds on D-641, D-459 · The first glass in chapter 2, then one more a chapter, each showing something different (e.g. hidden marks), swapped at home before a trip; what each shows is the owner's pick.
- **1.11 Each chapter's first floor bites** · builds on D-643, D-638, D-461 · A draft table of creature hearts and bite against the hero's metal (+1 a chapter), so a new chapter's top feels hard and its bottom feels earned.
- **1.12 Oil stays tight as tanks grow** · builds on D-014, D-641, D-642 · Tune each chapter so bigger tanks and better oil plants are matched by longer floors or darker places, so the oil worry never melts away by chapter 4.
- **1.13 Trips sized for a phone** · new: needs the owner's OK · A draft target of about 10 to 20 minutes for a trip from home down and back, using the waystones every few depths, checked by the pacing bot.

## Toward publishing
- **1.14 A whole-journey bot run** · builds on D-634, D-948, D-334 · Before 1.0, the bot plays all four chapters in Gentle, Normal and Hardcore, turning turns into rough hours to check the 12 to 20 hour aim and find any wall.
- **1.15 Testers' first-hour tuning** · builds on D-590, D-658 · With the 12 Play testers, note where each stops or falls in the first hour, and smooth only those spots: oil, the first ember, the first part.

Moved word for word from `OPEN-QUESTIONS.md` at 0.3.32, the eighth audit
(D-570), as it reached 8 KB: the questions that wait for outside places,
and swimming. Q-037 and Q-038, how regions join and the kinds of room,
moved here word for word at 0.3.152, the eleventh audit (D-699), and

**Q-027 · Darkness outside, and the moon** (when outside places exist)
Each place sets its own darkness (D-063). Outside at night, remembered
tiles use the study's cold blue as moonlight (D-217). Should moon phases
make some nights darker than others? The owner isn't sure the moon will
be in the game. Each biome's darkness is asked when it arrives.

**Q-034 · Trees outside** (when outside places exist)
The owner's notes on the woods sketch from 0.2.3
(https://claude.ai/artifact/NNdk6rD2Cgg1FkNguoaKg4): the trees need
more work. A lantern's shadow should follow the tree's shape, not
darken its whole tile. Later: several kinds of tree, more variety, and
maybe trees swaying in the wind. One round tree a tile came first
(D-209). How many kinds, and does wind come with them?

**Q-035 · Weather, and shadows from the sun and moon** (when outside places exist)
The owner's ideas: different kinds of weather, and outside shadows that
follow the time of day, cast from where the sun (or the moon, see Q-027)
is in the sky. Which weather, what does it change, and do shadows move
with the hour?

**Q-039 · Swimming** (later)
How the hero learns to swim, where they may jump into water, what water
does to the lantern and oil, and how rivers and underground streams
carry them into rooms reached no other way (D-171).

**Q-040 · Light through openings** (when outside places exist)
The cave mouth is settled (D-218). Still open: daylight down stairs from
outside, how weather dims it, and how strong the glow from an unfound
lit room is (D-173).

**Q-037 · How regions are generated and joined** (stage 0.3)
Proposed in `WORLD.md`: two passes, the region's layout first, then each
room made to fit its ways in, each room with its own seed. Built for
depth 2 (depth 3 before D-212; D-201), and for every floor under the
crypt since D-645; the owner left the rest open until a second region. How do
regions join each other: grid edge to grid edge on the surface, and
stairs or cave mouths between the surface and the regions below?

**Q-038 · Kinds of room, shapes and sizes** (stage 0.3)
Which kinds (tunnel, clearing, house, marsh, desert…), how big each is,
and whether shapes come from hand-made templates, are generated, or both
(D-172). The owner mentioned "predefined tiles" for the shapes. Rooms
of other sizes line up their stairs by place on the floor (D-204). The
crypt rooms are 11 × 13 (D-195); generated rooms are made of 11 × 13
cells, 13 × 15 from depth 8: 1 cell, a hall of 2 or a large room of
2 × 2 (D-645, D-648).
Narrowed by D-223: each kind of place has its own rules, and deeper
floors become grids; which rules each kind has is still open. Burrow
floors are warrens (D-227), and from depth 8 dungeon halls (D-228).
Sizes by depth for the caves, warrens and dungeon before D-645: D-580,
built in D-584.

**Q-089 · Falling down holes** (raised 2026-11-09, D-744) The owner
would allow a hole in the ground as a cave mouth only if holes or
chasms you can drop down to go deeper, as in some roguelikes, ever
come. Whether they do, where, and what a fall costs: open, for later.

**Q-088 · Campfires** (raised 2026-11-09, the owner's idea; talked
through after D-733's five steps, the owner's pick) In the owner's
words: "I also had a cool idea of having a chance to run into a
campfire that will vary on how much it is lit, which will dictate the
message that appears. Campfires that are out could potentially be
started with the lantern if you have oil in it, using one move of oil.
Perhaps even a way to add to the fire, or craft a fire yourself,
confurring some sort of buff when used. These are just ideas and they
need further discussion." Where they turn up, how lit each is and its
words, what lighting one costs, feeding one, building one and the buff
are all open. The first step, fires met in the world and lighting cold
ashes, is D-757; feeding one, building one and the buff stay open.

**Q-091 · Sleeping out's later costs** (raised 2026-11-09, the owner's
ideas, with D-758) In the owner's words: "Maybe at some point in the
future, they could even wake to a message saying something of theirs was
stolen, but with a side quest to get it back (if it was of high or
significant value)", and "Depending on the environment, there could be
other effects, such as a snowy environment causing the player to be
chilled." What can be taken and how often, who takes it, the quest to
get it back, and each place's own cost are all open, for once people
and quests grow (D-470) and other places are outside.

**Q-092 · Flowers to harvest** (raised 2026-11-09, the owner's addition
to D-760's gathering; the herbs outside came first, D-762) In the
owner's words: "Actual harvestable plants, and flowers.", "I want
flowers as well, which can be used in potion making.", "I think it would
depend on the type of plant and the place." and "there are going to be
more than one kind, and there needs to be variations in the way they
are drawn." Picked, a flower goes in the bag for crafts later (D-762).
The first is the Duskstar, in the woods rooms (D-763). The other kinds,
their names and looks, where each grows and which potions they make are
open, each at its own step.

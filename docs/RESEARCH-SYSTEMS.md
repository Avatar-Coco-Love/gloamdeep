# Research: the game's systems, looking ahead

The long-term talk of 2026-11-13 (D-889), kept so nothing is lost.
**Nothing here is decided** beyond what D-889 says; the proposals are
Claude's, kept by the owner, each built only by its own decision. The
puzzles from the same talk are in `PUZZLES.md`.

## Where the game stood (0.3.333)
Only biome 1 exists: the woods and mountains on the surface, and below
them the crypt, caves, warrens and dungeon (depth 8 down). 19 creatures,
3 places to make things (the bag, the workbench, the mortar), 4
draughts, 1 relic (the Locket), 1 dropped weapon (the Warden's Fist).
Biomes 2 to 4 (the mines, the fungal caverns, the sunken ruins) are
planned in shape (`SCOPE*.md`), not made.

## Crafting
- **Settled:** 7 craft families; about 8 to 12 new things to make a
  biome; one new metal a biome; better tools open new materials and
  places; later stations: a forge, a kitchen fire, a power bench, an
  altar (D-415, D-450, D-455, D-456).
- **Missing at 0.3.333:** smithing (copper is used, but no forge and no
  weapon tiers); the lantern's part slots (tank, wick and glass planned,
  D-459; only the wick exists); cooking; the electronic and arcane
  families.
- **Claude's proposal:** the **lantern part slots** first, the signature
  craft (pillar 1); then the **forge and copper weapons**, the same
  attack shape with harder hits. Together they make "deeper → find →
  craft → deeper" a felt loop.

## Enemies
Creatures are mixed from building blocks (`RESEARCH-2.md`): how it
moves, how it attacks, how it meets light, one trait. At 0.3.333:

| Part | Used | Not yet tried |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Moves | chases, lurks, burrows, shy, lures, never moves | keeps away (kites), teleports |
| Attacks | bite, strike in a line, spit from range, slam with knockback, drinks oil, poisons | area burst, delayed blast, snuffs the lantern |
| Light | shuns it, seeks it, glows | eats light, can only be hit while lit |
| Traits | packs, bones that rise again, disguises | splits, armoured in front, heals others |

- **Claude's proposal:** every new creature fills one empty cell; each
  biome bends one rule (the fungal caverns light themselves); above
  them, 4 biome bosses and then Old Maren, each fought in phases that
  test what was learned (Q-083, D-634).
- **The gap:** biome 1 has no boss and no found ability yet, though
  D-463 promises one each a biome. That is the biggest gap before
  biome 2.

## Other big mechanics already on the list
Boons at shrines; campfires (Q-088); weather (Q-035); rooms that fill
again (Q-086); rescued people and villages below; the bow (Q-094); the
"Stories" mode (`RESEARCH.md`). Claude's suggestion: these stay queued
behind biome 1's boss, found ability, first puzzles and lantern parts.

## The talk's questions and the owner's answers
Asked as multiple choice; the answers are D-889.
- **Plan first:** puzzles (recommended), the owner adding "I have so many
  ideas for puzzles, new mechanics, time constraints, high pressure
  moments". Offered too: biome 1's boss and ability; crafting tiers; the
  enemy toolkit.
- **Puzzle kinds:** all four: light, push, tool and ability gates,
  creature puzzles; "there are many more yet to be listed".
- **Where:** all four: sealed side vaults (recommended), small
  everywhere, terminal mini-games, landmark set pieces; "many, many more
  to come".
- **Order:** finish biome 1 first (recommended): its boss, found
  ability, first puzzles and lantern parts before the mines. Offered
  too: start biome 2 soon; a vertical slice.
- Then the owner asked for many ideas to choose from or change
  (`PUZZLES*.md`), and kept every one.

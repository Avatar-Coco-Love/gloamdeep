# Roadmap to 1.0

The owner's picks on the planning board, 2026-11-20 (D-998): the 97 kept
ideas, by horizon, in build order. A line is `number name (size)`; the
idea's full text is in `planning/` (grep the number), its check in
`planning/CHECK-*.md`. Sizes: S small, M medium, L large. Kept means
"we will do this"; names, numbers and story in an idea are still drafts,
each the owner's pick at its own step, proposed first as always. The
order may change. Maybes and drops: `RESEARCH-PLANNING.md`.

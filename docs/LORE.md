## The story, oldest first
1. **Underhearth and the First Light.** A city, Underhearth, stood deep
   under the ground, where the sunken ruins are now. Its people knew the
   gloam is alive and eats light. To hold it back they built a great
   lamp, the First Light, and the Lightkeepers kept it burning.
2. **The fall.** The city burned the First Light's oil faster than the
   deep could make it. The Light dimmed, the gloam got in, and
   Underhearth fell. Survivors climbed up carrying one spark of it,
   lit the hearth with it and built Hearthwood around it. The
   Lightkeepers sealed their old road down and laid their dead in the
   crypt on top of it, like a lid.
3. **The miners.** Generations later, the stories had faded. Miners
   digging for copper broke into the deep by another way. The gloam
   spreads tales of treasure as bait, because people carry light down.
   The treasure is real; the tales are the trap. The peddler was one of
   those miners: he knows the way down and regrets it.
4. **The call.** The gloam rises and lights fail on the surface. The
   dying First Light sends up a call that Lightkeepers can hear, and
   sparks, small living lights, to lead them down.
5. **Old Maren.** The hero's teacher, Old Maren, heard the call and went
   down, and the hero went too. Maren's journal pages, left floor by
   floor, are the lore pages found. Deep down, Maren's oil ran out and
   the gloam took them. The pages stop.
6. **Now.** The gloam took the hero's memories with their teacher, and
   the hearth pulled the hero home to the crypt. The hero has forgotten.
   The Lightkeepers dug Maren an empty grave there; it hurts before the
   hero knows why, and the journal slowly shows the hero was there.
7. **The goal.** Follow the call all the way down and relight the First
   Light, so the gloam is pushed back. The journey goes on after it: the gloam
   is pushed back but not gone (D-634).

## Old Maren, taken by the gloam
When a Lightkeeper's oil runs out in the deep, the gloam remakes them
as a gloamling. Maren went further than anyone, and became something
far worse: a boss with a history (Q-083). When the hero beats Maren,
Maren's lantern relights; for a moment they are themselves again, and
pass something on before they go. The fight comes near the end, deep in
Underhearth, just before the First Light (D-634).

## How the world works
- **Death sends you home:** every Lightkeeper is bound to the hearth,
  lit from the First Light. When the hero's light fails below, the
  hearth pulls them home before the gloam can finish. The ember left
  when the oil runs out is the gloam eating light, and memory with it.
- **The finds:** oil flasks, herbs and other finds are left by one lone
  keeper, never caught, always a room ahead. Who the keeper is waits
  (Q-084).
- **Mimics:** the gloam's bait, copying the keeper's gifts.
- **Gloamlings:** Lightkeepers whose oil ran out. They crave the light
  they lost, but it burns them, so they wait just past the lantern and
  grow bolder as it dims.
- **The guide** (answers Q-072): a spark of the First Light, a small warm
  glow beside the hero, freed from a trap early on. It never lights
  rooms; the lantern stays the only light (pillar 1). It brightens
  nearer the First Light and dims near the gloam. Later the hero meets a
  gloamling that remembers: a special wanderer that keeps to the edge of
  the light and speaks in broken pieces.
- **Waystones:** posts on the Lightkeepers' old road, each with a
  lamplighter's name carved on it. The deepest one found carries
  Maren's mark, the last post they kept.
- **Twisted portals:** warped worlds, some the gloam's dreams of what it
  has eaten; where its dreams run deep enough they touch other worlds
  entirely, and strange creatures and biomes come through.
- **Other creatures:** rats, spiders, bats, lampmoths, oil ticks and the
  rest are part of the world, not the gloam's doing.

## The ways down
The crypt is the oldest way down, the Lightkeepers' sealed road, but one
of many. The mines are the miners' way in. Other regions, villages, cave
mouths and structures on the surface each lead down through their own
biomes. All the deep ways meet near Underhearth at the bottom.

## Names

(the great lamp at the bottom), the keeper (who leaves the finds).

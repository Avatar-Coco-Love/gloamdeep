# Puzzles, part 3

`PUZZLES.md` continued, under its rules (D-889): kept is not built, and
every name and number is a placeholder until its own step.

## T · Time and pressure
The owner's high-pressure moments and time limits. Each keeps turns
waiting for you but puts a count on the room, so every move matters.

**T1 · The gloam tide** The gloam creeps in from one edge of the room a
row at a time. Standing in it drains oil fast, or hurts. Solve the room,
grab the loot and get out before it reaches the door. The lantern pushes
it back a little, buying a turn or two at the cost of oil. *Uses* oil,
the lore of the spreading gloam. *Needs* a creeping gloam tile. *Fits*
vaults in any biome.

**T2 · The burning fuse** The first brazier lit starts a count shown in
its flame. Each brazier burns for 8 turns, so all must be lit before the
first goes out. The room's layout and creatures decide the route. *Uses*
braziers (L1). *Needs* braziers that burn out. *Fits* after L1.

**T3 · Rising water** Pulling a lever opens the vault and starts a flood.
Water spreads tile by tile from the low side; it snuffs a set torch, and
wading slows you. Get the loot and out, or find the valve that stops it.
*Uses* water, torches. *Needs* spreading water, a lever. *Fits* the
sunken ruins, flooded mines.

**T4 · The collapse** Taking a relic shakes the room. Rubble falls behind
you each turn, closing off paths, and you run a short route to the
stairs or a doorway. Rubble already exists; here it falls. *Uses*
rubble, relics. *Needs* falling rubble with a warning shadow a turn
before. *Fits* the mines.

**T5 · The oil door** An old Lightkeeper lamp by a vault door drinks oil.
Pour 2 flasks into it and the door opens: you pay for the loot with your
own light, and choose when the trip is worth it. Low on oil, come back
later. *Uses* flasks, pouring. *Needs* an oil socket. *Fits* biome 1,
from the crypt.

**T6 · The dark crossing** A gallery of sleeping creatures that wake if
lit. See the room once in the lantern's light, then cross it with the
lantern off, from memory, through the fog of war. Pillar 1 turned upside
down. *Uses* the lantern switch, sleepers, the fog of war. *Needs* a
"wakes in light" sleeper. *Fits* the warrens.

**T7 · Hunted** A creature you can't beat follows you through a run of
rooms, a step behind. You lose it only by reaching light: a lit brazier,
a waystone or the surface. A high-pressure moment and a story beat; it
could be one of the unique wanderers (Q-083). *Uses* rooms, doorways,
lit places. *Needs* a creature that follows between rooms. *Fits* a set
piece, once per biome.

**T8 · Keep the lamppost lit till dawn** On the surface at night, relight
the dead lamppost and keep it burning until morning while night
creatures try to snuff it. A defend-the-light moment. *Uses* the
lamppost, night creatures, oil. *Needs* a creature that snuffs lights.
*Fits* the surface.

**T9 · Gusts** A draught blows through the room every few turns, shown a
turn ahead by dust on the floor. It snuffs set torches and, without a
glass on the lantern, shrinks its reach for a turn. Time your moves
between gusts; a later lantern glass part could block it. *Uses*
torches, the lantern's parts (D-459). *Needs* a wind that comes and
goes. *Fits* the mountain rooms, deep shafts.

In chat, the same talk also offered **an oil-fed vault** (T5) and
**a collapse as you run for the stairs** (T4) as the first pressure
ideas, with the gloam tide, the fuse and rising water.

## W · Bigger shapes
How puzzles join up across rooms and across the journey.

**W1 · A switch in one room, a door in the next** The owner wants part of
a room cut off, reached through another room (`RESEARCH.md`). A lever or
plate in one room opens a gate, or drains water, in the next; you hear
it open from the other side. *Uses* regions as grids of rooms (D-169).
*Needs* links between rooms. *Fits* hand-made regions, then generated
ones.

**W2 · Trial portals** Every biome has portals into a twisted version of
itself (D-032). One kind could be a trial: a chain of 3 to 5 generated
puzzle rooms, each harder, with a rare finer find at the end. Fail and
you're thrown out, as any portal does. *Uses* portal depths, rare finer
versions (D-415). *Needs* generated puzzle rooms, the solver. *Fits*
later in stage 0.3 or after.

**W3 · Puzzles that change the world for good** Some big puzzles leave a
lasting mark: a drained lake opens a cave floor, a relit district of
braziers keeps a room lit forever, the way back up becomes a trail of
your own light ("lit torches stay lit", `RESEARCH-2.md`). *Uses* one
persistent world (D-019). *Needs* saved world changes. *Fits* landmark
set pieces.

**W4 · The boss as the final exam** Biome 1's boss (not decided yet)
asks for each puzzle verb learned on the way down. For example, phase 1:
light the braziers to make it hittable; phase 2: knock it into water
with the Fist; phase 3: go dark to dodge what it does in the light. Fits
"bosses in phases that test what you know" (`RESEARCH-2.md`). *Uses*
the boss notes, Q-083. *Needs* its own design talk. *Fits* biome 1's
bottom.

## Each biome's own puzzle mechanics
Each biome bends one rule (an idea kept in `RESEARCH-2.md`); each list
gives that biome's puzzles a different feel.
- **Biome 1, the crypt, caves, warrens and dungeon:** Lightkeeper
  braziers and sealed doors (L1); dark-only runes (L3); the Fist and
  plates (P1); webs that burn (G1); statues that move in the dark (C4).
- **Biome 2, the old mines:** **firedamp**, pockets of gas that blow up
  near a lit flame, so the lantern must go dark to pass, or you light it
  on purpose to blast rubble; minecarts on rails, pushed to roll and
  smash; lifts and pulleys between levels; pumps against flooding; old
  wires, the start of the electronic craft.
- **Biome 3, the glowing fungal caverns:** the caverns light themselves,
  so the lantern matters less and darkness is rare; spores that burst
  when lit; mushrooms that grow toward light, making bridges;
  light-eating creatures; slick slime floors (P4).
- **Biome 4, the sunken ruins:** valves that raise and lower the water
  across rooms; great lens arrays carrying the First Light's beam (L2);
  Underhearth's machines and codes (K1, K2); tides that come and go by
  the turn.

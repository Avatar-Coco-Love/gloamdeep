# Vision

What Gloamdeep is reaching for. Only the parts marked **decided** are
settled. Everything else is a draft for the owner to approve.

## Pitch (draft)
Carry a lantern down through the gloam. Light is how you see the way, the
danger and the oil that keeps you going, and every lit turn burns a little
of it.

## Decided
- Name: Gloamdeep (D-001).
- Genre: a grid-based, turn-based survival micro-RPG with action and
  strategy (D-002).
- Phone first, any browser, everything drawn in code (D-003).
- The look comes from style study 1 (D-005, `ART-DIRECTION.md`), and all of
  it stays (D-009).
- Hosted on GitHub Pages (D-010).
- Built-in dev tools, including spawning anything (D-006).
- Four design pillars (D-013, below).
- Oil is light only. Running dry leaves an ember and bolder enemies, not
  the end of the run. The lantern switches on and off (D-014, D-015).
- Turns pass only when you act. Bump to attack, plus ranged abilities,
  weapons and area attacks (D-016, D-017).
- One persistent journey in a world generated once per save, with portals
  into deep areas that change on every visit (D-019).
- Death sends you home and never wipes progress (D-020, D-021).
- Surface first, then down; caves can open into valleys (D-022).
- Solo, with a rescued guide joining early, not a faery: what it is waits (D-023, D-034, D-508, Q-072).
- Every biome has portals into a twisted version of itself (D-032).
- The hero is a lamplighter; why they go down is uncovered slowly (D-036).
- Biomes, top to bottom: woods and mountains, old mines and caves, glowing
  fungal caverns, sunken ruins (D-028).

## Inspirations
- **Dungeon Siege.** The owner played it as a kid, and the name brings it
  to mind. What to take from it (D-008): going deeper, the feeling of
  exploring, collecting loot, fighting new monsters, uncovering mysteries
  and lore, getting stronger and progressing slowly, seeing how far you can
  get, and the curiosity that makes you keep playing.
- **Style study 1.** The lantern as the only light you carry, and the room
  waking up as you walk through it. The owner kept every part of it
  (D-009): real light and shadows, stepped dithered light, fog of war,
  brick wall faces, crisp pixels, the palette and the small signs of life.

## Pillars
**Decided** (D-013). Every later idea is checked against these. An idea
that serves none of them is cut.

1. **The lantern is your world.** The lantern is the only light you
   carry. Whatever it shows you, the path, the danger or the treasure, is
   the game, and the dark beyond it should feel alive. (From D-009.)
2. **Always one floor deeper.** Going down is the pull. Each descent
   should feel like a step into somewhere new, and "how far can I get?"
   should always be on your mind. (From D-008.)
3. **Curiosity pays off.** Looking around is always worth it. Corners and
   side rooms hold loot, new monsters and pieces of the mystery. (From
   D-008.)
4. **Stronger, slowly.** You get stronger bit by bit and earn it, and you
   can feel the difference between your first steps and your fiftieth.
   (From D-008.)

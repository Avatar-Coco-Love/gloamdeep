**D-450 · 2026-10-30 · The scope, part 2: ore and tools** (the owner's
picks; builds D-413, D-414, D-282) One new metal per biome for now, each
a step better than the one above; more may come later. Better picks open
the next ore, as in Stardew Valley; stone stays breakable by hand.
Better axes open the deeper woods. Tools open places too *(e.g. a pick
through a cracked wall, a rod across water)*. Names and numbers come at
each one's own step. Also moved D-441 to D-448 to the archive, word for
word, as this file neared 12 KB.

**D-451 · 2026-10-30 · The scope, part 2: plants, wood and oil** (the
owner's picks, all recommended) Each biome adds 2 or 3 plants to gather,
one of them an oil plant like the oilcap, deeper ones giving more oil.
Plain wood up top; each deeper biome has one wood of its own, opened by
a better axe. Stone stays plain everywhere; a biome stone may come later.
New things grow back at today's two speeds (D-306): plants 300 turns,
trees and deposits 900, rarer things slower.

**D-452 · 2026-10-30 · The scope, part 2: fishing and farming** (the
owner's picks) Fishing works in any water, on the surface and below,
with the right tools. Farming is a garden at home: seeds planted on the
surface grow over turns while the hero is below. Gardening away from
home may come later. How each works is asked at its own step.

**D-453 · 2026-10-30 · The scope, part 3: drop chances** (the owner's
picks, all recommended; builds D-417, narrows Q-061) A beaten creature
drops its common material about 1 time in 2. Creatures from depth 3 down
(flask mimic, scorpion, gloamling) also have a rare one, about 1 in 10;
rats and lampmoths have only a common one. A drop lies where the
creature fell, taken by stepping on it; a full bag leaves it there. The
chances stay fixed for now; better odds in portal depths, or a charm or
Growth upgrade that raises them, may come later.

**D-454 · 2026-10-30 · The scope, part 3: today's materials** (the
owner's picks, all recommended; narrows Q-061) Rat: Rat Tail. Lampmoth:
Moth Dust. Scorpion: Stinger, rare Venom Sac. Flask mimic: Mimic Glass,
rare Mimic Heart. Gloamling: Gloam Wisp, rare Gloam Eye. Each leans to
what fits its creature: Moth Dust and Gloam Wisp to lampcraft, Stinger
and Venom Sac to weapons and antidotes, Rat Tail and Mimic Glass to
leatherwork and flasks, the rare ones to finer gear. Recipes are part 4.

**D-455 · 2026-10-30 · The scope, part 4: where things are made** (the
owner's picks, all recommended; builds D-296, D-415) Small things, such
as flasks, potions and torches, are made in the bag, anywhere. Building,
tools and leatherwork are made at the workbench. Later each bigger
family gets one station of its own, built at home: a forge for
smithing, a kitchen fire for cooking, a power bench for electronic, an
altar for arcane. Stations stand only on the surface, like the
workbench, so coming home to make things is part of the loop. Each
station's name, look and cost are settled at its own step.

**D-456 · 2026-10-30 · The scope, part 4: the ladder** (the owner's
picks, all recommended; builds D-414, D-418) Each biome has about 8 to
12 new things to make. Most rungs open by their materials: a recipe
shows once the hero has held its parts, so deeper materials, opened by
better tools (D-450), open deeper recipes. A few special recipes, about
1 or 2 a biome, the rarer or finer things, are found below instead, as
are the electronic and arcane stations themselves (D-415). Stations
don't improve for now; whether they can is asked with home upgrades in
part 8. The owner asked to weigh station upgrades and found recipes
before picking these.

**D-450 · 2026-10-30 · The scope, part 2: ore and tools** (the owner's
picks; builds D-413, D-414, D-282) One new metal per biome for now, each
a step better than the one above; more may come later. Better picks open
the next ore, as in Stardew Valley; stone stays breakable by hand.
Better axes open the deeper woods. Tools open places too *(e.g. a pick
through a cracked wall, a rod across water)*. Names and numbers come at
each one's own step. Also moved D-441 to D-448 to the archive, word for
word, as this file neared 12 KB.

**D-451 · 2026-10-30 · The scope, part 2: plants, wood and oil** (the
owner's picks, all recommended) Each biome adds 2 or 3 plants to gather,
one of them an oil plant like the oilcap, deeper ones giving more oil.
Plain wood up top; each deeper biome has one wood of its own, opened by
a better axe. Stone stays plain everywhere; a biome stone may come later.
New things grow back at today's two speeds (D-306): plants 300 turns,
trees and deposits 900, rarer things slower.

**D-452 · 2026-10-30 · The scope, part 2: fishing and farming** (the
owner's picks) Fishing works in any water, on the surface and below,
with the right tools. Farming is a garden at home: seeds planted on the
surface grow over turns while the hero is below. Gardening away from
home may come later. How each works is asked at its own step.

**D-453 · 2026-10-30 · The scope, part 3: drop chances** (the owner's
picks, all recommended; builds D-417, narrows Q-061) A beaten creature
drops its common material about 1 time in 2. Creatures from depth 3 down
(flask mimic, scorpion, gloamling) also have a rare one, about 1 in 10;
rats and lampmoths have only a common one. A drop lies where the
creature fell, taken by stepping on it; a full bag leaves it there. The
chances stay fixed for now; better odds in portal depths, or a charm or
Growth upgrade that raises them, may come later.

**D-454 · 2026-10-30 · The scope, part 3: today's materials** (the
owner's picks, all recommended; narrows Q-061) Rat: Rat Tail. Lampmoth:
Moth Dust. Scorpion: Stinger, rare Venom Sac. Flask mimic: Mimic Glass,
rare Mimic Heart. Gloamling: Gloam Wisp, rare Gloam Eye. Each leans to
what fits its creature: Moth Dust and Gloam Wisp to lampcraft, Stinger
and Venom Sac to weapons and antidotes, Rat Tail and Mimic Glass to
leatherwork and flasks, the rare ones to finer gear. Recipes are part 4.

**D-455 · 2026-10-30 · The scope, part 4: where things are made** (the
owner's picks, all recommended; builds D-296, D-415) Small things, such
as flasks, potions and torches, are made in the bag, anywhere. Building,
tools and leatherwork are made at the workbench. Later each bigger
family gets one station of its own, built at home: a forge for
smithing, a kitchen fire for cooking, a power bench for electronic, an
altar for arcane. Stations stand only on the surface, like the
workbench, so coming home to make things is part of the loop. Each
station's name, look and cost are settled at its own step.

**D-456 · 2026-10-30 · The scope, part 4: the ladder** (the owner's
picks, all recommended; builds D-414, D-418) Each biome has about 8 to
12 new things to make. Most rungs open by their materials: a recipe
shows once the hero has held its parts, so deeper materials, opened by
better tools (D-450), open deeper recipes. A few special recipes, about
1 or 2 a biome, the rarer or finer things, are found below instead, as
are the electronic and arcane stations themselves (D-415). Stations
don't improve for now; whether they can is asked with home upgrades in
part 8. The owner asked to weigh station upgrades and found recipes
before picking these.

**D-457 · 2026-10-30 · The scope, part 5: weapons** (the owner's picks,
all recommended; builds D-027, D-252, D-450) About 4 or 5 weapon kinds,
each with its own attack shape *(e.g. a knife hitting 1 tile, a spear 2
in a line, an axe a sweep of 3, a sling at range)*. Each biome's new
metal makes a better one of each kind: the shape stays, the hits grow.
The hero holds one weapon in hand, as today; others ride in the bag and
are swapped there, a swap taking a turn. Each kind's name, shape and
numbers come at its own step. Also moved D-450 to D-454 to the archive,
word for word, as this file neared 12 KB.

**D-458 · 2026-10-30 · The scope, part 5: armour and charms** (the
owner's picks, recommended; builds D-415) Besides the weapon in hand,
the hero wears one armour piece, made by leatherwork, and two charms,
each with one small effect *(e.g. better drop odds, a slower burn)*.
Each piece's effect and recipe come at its own step.

**D-459 · 2026-10-30 · The scope, part 5: the lantern's parts and
torches** (the owner's picks, all recommended; the owner's thought of
many lantern upgrades) The lantern has a few part slots *(e.g. tank,
wick, glass)*, with many parts for each; one fits per slot and can be
swapped *(e.g. a bigger tank holds more oil, a slow wick burns less, a
coloured glass shows hidden marks)*. Oil still alone sets how far the
light reaches (D-014). Parts are made at the workbench (answering part
4's open point) and fitted from the bag, anywhere. The other light in
the plan is torches, made in the bag and set down on a tile, burning
for a while (D-024, D-455). Flares, glowing gear and machine lights were
weighed and left out for now; any may come back by a new decision.

**D-460 · 2026-10-30 · The scope, part 5: the rod** (the owner's pick,
recommended; builds D-450, D-452) Fishing uses one rod, made better
with each biome's metal like picks and axes; a better rod catches
deeper fish. Bait may come later. How fishing plays is asked at its own
step.

**D-461 · 2026-10-30 · The scope, part 6: levels and embers** (the
owner's pick, recommended; builds D-246, D-293, D-371) Levels keep
today's curve, each costing 10 more experience than the one before, and
each still gives 1 ember, with no cap. Deeper creatures give more
experience by themselves (hearts and bite), so the climb stays steady. Also moved D-455 and D-456 to the archive,
word for word, as this file passed 12 KB.

**D-462 · 2026-10-30 · The scope, part 6: the Growth tree** (the owner's
picks, all recommended; builds D-293, D-419) The Growth tree has three
branches: Light *(e.g. a slower burn, a bigger tank)*, Body (today's
hearts, and *e.g.* a stronger bite, a guard) and Hands *(e.g. more bag
slots, faster gathering, better drop odds)*. Gathering skills are Hands
upgrades bought with embers, not learned by doing. Each branch's next
row opens once the hero has reached the next biome, so going deeper
opens stronger upgrades; each upgrade costs 1 ember, as today. Each
upgrade and its numbers come at its own step.

**D-463 · 2026-10-30 · The scope, part 6: found abilities** (the owner's
pick, recommended; builds D-419) Each biome hides one big ability, at a
shrine or from a boss near its bottom. Each opens paths in places
already seen *(e.g. a leap over a 1-tile gap)*, so going back pays
(pillar 3). Which ability lies where comes at its own step.

**D-464 · 2026-10-30 · The scope, part 7: lore** (the owner's pick,
recommended; builds D-038, D-414) Lore pieces are notes, carvings and
murals found below. They fill a Lore tab in the Journal, each telling a
bit of the mystery. They come in sets *(e.g. 5 pages of one story)*;
finishing a set gives one small lasting boost. The sets, their stories
and boosts come at each one's own step. Also moved D-457 to D-460 to
the archive, word for word, as this file neared 12 KB.

**D-465 · 2026-10-30 · The scope, part 7: relics** (the owner's pick,
recommended; builds D-038, D-419) Relics are rare finds, about 1 or 2 a
biome besides its found ability (D-463). Each is set on a shelf at home
and gives one small lasting boost while it is there, so home grows with
what was found. Each relic and its boost come at its own step.

**D-466 · 2026-10-30 · The scope, part 7: trophies** (the owner's pick,
recommended; builds D-350, D-417) Each creature has one trophy, made at
the workbench from its materials and hung at home. It completes that
creature's Bestiary page. Trophies are for looks and pride and give no
strength. Each trophy's recipe and look come at its own step.

**D-467 · 2026-10-30 · The scope, part 7: achievements** (the owner's
pick, recommended; builds D-284, D-418, narrows Q-057) Each chosen
record has a set of 3 tiers, and each tier pays the look currency. They
show in a Feats tab in the Journal, with a note on reaching one. Which
records get a set, and each tier's number, come at the first one's step.

**D-469 · 2026-10-30 · The scope, part 8: home** (the owner's picks;
builds D-038, D-455, D-465) Home grows by the pieces built there with
the Building craft *(e.g. a bed, shelves, the stations)*, with no home
level. Stations still don't improve (D-456). Sleeping at home gives a
short boost for the next trip *(e.g. a little extra oil or a heart)*.
Each piece and the boost come at each one's own step.

**D-470 · 2026-10-30 · The scope, part 8: people** (the owner's picks,
the recommended one and the owner's addition; builds the wish in
`RESEARCH.md`) Most people are rescued below and move home, each giving
it a use *(e.g. a vendor, a teacher)*. Later, some people stay where the
hero meets them *(e.g. the cult in a lit place below)*, and wanderers
visit *(e.g. a wandering merchant)*. Who each is comes at its own step.

**D-471 · 2026-10-30 · The scope, part 8: the first vendor** (the
owner's pick, recommended) The first vendor is a rescued person who sets
up at home. They buy creature materials and spare finds and sell useful
things *(e.g. seeds, recipes, basics)*. More vendors come with more
rescues. Who they are and their prices come at their own step.

**D-472 · 2026-10-30 · The scope, part 8: trade coins** (the owner's
picks, recommended, and kept on falling added after the PR opened;
builds D-418) Trade coins come only from selling to vendors: creatures
never drop them and none are found below, so finds stay the reward.
Coins are kept when the hero falls. The look currency stays earned by deeds only (Q-055).

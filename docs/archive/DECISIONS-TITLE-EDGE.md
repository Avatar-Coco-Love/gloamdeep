# The title and the room's edge (D-401 to D-403, D-405)

Moved unchanged from `docs/DECISIONS.md` at 0.2.157 (D-409), when it
neared 12 KB: the title like a phone game's and its icons, and the room's
edge going on past a small room. These are still in force unless marked
superseded. Grep for an ID or topic; never read this whole.

---

**D-401 · 2026-10-28 · The title like a phone game's** (the owner's
picks, [mock-up](https://claude.ai/artifact/Bgi4tPb69ZKLznwbDKKXP8),
[drawn by the game](https://claude.ai/artifact/51txhJqL3rYHLrdwDQXtr8))
Layout A: the hero in the lantern's light in the middle, one big ember
button under them, CONTINUE while a journey is saved with New journey a
grey line under it, else NEW JOURNEY alone. Journal, How to play and
Settings are round glass buttons top right, a 5 × 5 icon in each (a book,
a question mark, a gear), its name under it; on a squarer screen they go
down the right. The profile in play is a glass chip top left, its hero,
name and deepest depth; a tap opens Profiles, so the Profiles line is
gone. The Seed Explorer is a round button bottom left, a wrench, its name
beside it. The name is lit from below at triple size, picked from six
drawings (supersedes D-186's carved look and double size): dim stone at
the letters' tops to gold and bone at their feet, the dark outline and
ember edge kept. Two steps: this, with draft icons, built in 0.2.152;
then the icons tuned after playing it. D-393 to D-400 moved unchanged to
`archive/DECISIONS-CARDS-2.md`. Its 5 × 5 icons superseded by D-402
(2026-10-28).

**D-402 · 2026-10-28 · The title's icons** (the owner's picks, both
recommended, [mock-up](https://claude.ai/artifact/6dBqbqFa8xDYXoSnzCFEyH);
supersedes D-401's 5 × 5 icons) The round buttons' icons are 9 × 9, drawn
at one game pixel per dot like the hero and the hearts, the same size on
screen as the drafts: an open book with lines (Journal), a round question
mark (How to play), a gear with a hole (Settings) and a wrench with an
open jaw (Seed Explorer). Stone grey, gold when chosen, as before. Built
in 0.2.153.

**D-403 · 2026-10-28 · Past a small room's edge** (the owner's pick,
recommended; changes D-381's "deepest dark around it") Where a room is
narrower or shorter than the screen, whatever stands at its edge (rock in
a cave, trees outside) goes on past it all around, lit like the room, so
dark stays dark, daylight carries on, and no seam or flat colour shows.
It can't be walked on. Later, the owner's idea: a room on the same depth
behind the wall shows there instead, once visited or while something in
it shines (Q-066). Built in 0.2.155 (D-405).

**D-405 · 2026-10-28 · How the edge goes on** (the owner's picks, both
recommended, from pictures drawn by the game; builds D-403) Past the edge
the room's own rock, brick or trees go on, each as remembered as the edge
tile nearest it, so remembered ground carries on dim and unseen stays
dark; the lantern doesn't reach through the rock. Straight out from each
doorway a floor passage runs on, lit as the lantern nears. Behind the
woods' cave mouth the trees go on, as it leads down into the rock (not
asked yet, the build's own choice; the owner may change it). Built in
0.2.155.

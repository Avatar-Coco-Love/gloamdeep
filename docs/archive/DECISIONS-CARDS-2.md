# The menu cards, part 2 (D-393 to D-400)

Moved unchanged from `docs/DECISIONS.md` at 0.2.152 (D-401), when it
neared 12 KB: Version history, the seed pad, the map, the way to play, the
profiles, the name pad, the looks and the asks as cards, all built. These
are still in force unless marked superseded. Grep for an ID or topic;
never read this whole.

---

**D-393 · 2026-10-28 · Version history as a card** (the owner's picks,
both recommended, [mock-up](https://claude.ai/artifact/5wfbN8oH3FUVpGcxiu2snN))
Version history opens as a glass card like the settings card (D-389),
VERSION HISTORY in ember top left, from Settings on the room and on the
title's scene alike. Its list stays one list as on the page (D-254): each
version's number, title and + or −, an open one's date and lines under
its row, wrapped to the card's width. It scrolls inside the card, the
thin bar kept at its right. The round cross goes back to Settings, as Esc
and a tap outside do, so the Back line is gone. Built in 0.2.144.

**D-394 · 2026-10-28 · The seed pad as a card** (the owner's picks, both
recommended, [mock-up](https://claude.ai/artifact/3Sqb5FWCeDRPhisHUKs2YF))
The seed pad opens as a glass card like the settings card (D-389), SEED
in ember top left, from Settings and from the Seed Explorer alike. The
digits typed sit in an ember box, the keys under it as buttons like Make
on the pad's 4-column grid, both centred in the card; the chosen key ember,
the rest dim. The keys and how arrows move between them stay as they are
(D-191). The round cross goes back, as Esc and a tap outside do. Built in
0.2.145.

**D-395 · 2026-10-28 · The map as a card** (the owner's picks, all
recommended, [mock-up](https://claude.ai/artifact/2tpkiwwgPRNCUyDwKcmQ7e))
The map opens as a glass card like the bag's (D-385), MAP in ember top
left, the floor's name and its arrows centred on the same row (D-206), the
round cross closing it as M, Esc and a tap outside do; the round map
(D-383) hides while it is open. The Seed Explorer (D-214) is the same card
on the title's scene, Back, Type and Reseed buttons like Make along its
foot, the seed in gold between them. Scrolling, zooming and stairs stay as
they are. A bug found on the way is fixed too: the map turns up to the
surface again. Built in 0.2.146.

**D-396 · 2026-10-28 · The way to play as a card** (the owner's picks,
all recommended, [mock-up](https://claude.ai/artifact/5E2ooeSYUHJ1cLRh2kvW3b))
The ways to play (D-334) open as a glass card like the settings card
(D-389), WAY TO PLAY in ember top left, from New journey on the title's
scene and from Settings' Way to play line in play alike (MO4, D-346). Each
way is a row, its name and its note under it, so all three notes show at
once; the chosen row gold with the flame. A way that can't be picked stays
grey with its own note. Tapping a way or its note picks it. The round
cross goes back, as Esc and a tap outside do. The ask before an easier way
stays as it is until the asks become cards. Built in 0.2.147.

**D-397 · 2026-10-28 · The profiles as a card** (the owner's picks, all
recommended, [mock-up](https://claude.ai/artifact/NZyy1uTEaFYbMVxMpsu81X))
The slot screen (D-271) opens as a glass card like the settings card
(D-389) on the title's scene, PROFILES in ember top left. The three framed
boxes stay as they are (D-287, D-290). Rename, Change look and Delete
(D-273, D-289) are buttons like the Explorer's (D-395) along its foot:
RENAME, LOOK and DELETE, the one being done reading CANCEL while the
card's title asks which. The round cross goes back to the title, as Esc
and a tap outside do; when the game first opens there is no title yet, so
the cross is hidden, as Back was. The Delete question stays a page until
the asks become cards. Built in 0.2.148.

**D-398 · 2026-10-28 · The name pad as a card** (the owner's picks, all
recommended) The name pad (D-272) opens as a glass card like the seed
card (D-394) on the title's scene, NAME in ember top left, or RENAME when
renaming a profile (D-273). The letters typed sit in an ember box, the
keys under it as buttons like Make on the pad's 7-column grid, DEL and OK
wider on the last row, both centred in the card; the chosen key ember, the
rest dim. The round cross goes back to the profiles, as Esc and a tap
outside do, so the Back key is gone (supersedes D-288). Built in 0.2.149.

**D-399 · 2026-10-28 · The looks as cards** (the owner's picks, all
recommended, [mock-up](https://claude.ai/artifact/KVRN72rzpnRRZPo6BfoksW))
The cloak, eyes, trim and clothes screens (P3 to P6) open as glass cards
like the name card (D-398) on the title's scene, CLOAK, EYES, TRIM and
CLOTHES in ember top left. The framed boxes stay as they are, centred in
the card, the picked one gold. OK is a button in the bottom right corner.
The round cross goes back a card, as Back did, and so does a tap outside,
so the Back line is gone; Esc stays as it was. Built in 0.2.150.

**D-400 · 2026-10-28 · The asks as cards** (the owner's picks, all
recommended) Start over? (D-238), Delete …? (D-271) and Play …?, the ask
before an easier way (D-346), open as a small glass card centred on the
title's scene or the room, which shows dim around it. The question in
ember top left like the other cards' names, the round cross top right,
the grey note centred under the rule, YES and NO as buttons like Make
(D-299) along its foot, NO picked first. The cross, Esc and a tap outside
mean No. Your journey ends. (D-344) stays a page for now. Built in 0.2.151.

**D-474 · 2026-10-30 · The Rat Tail** (the owner's picks, all
recommended; step 1 of D-473, narrows Q-061) On the floor and in the
bag it is look A: the rat's pink tail curled into a loop, with a grey
stub where it joined the rat. The 1-in-2 coin is seeded like a flee's,
from the seed, the turn and the tile the rat fell on, so nothing new is
saved and a replay drops the same. Stacks of 20, as other small finds
(D-333). Three lines when taken and three when chosen in the bag, each
in turn, and a "can't carry more" line, drafted for the playtest. Left on the floor it
stays until taken and never grows back.

**D-475 · 2026-10-30 · The other four common drops** (the owner's
picks, all recommended; step 2 of D-473, narrows Q-061) Each is look A
of the drops mock-up: Moth Dust a small heap of pale dust with amber
glints and two motes; the Stinger the scorpion's tail tip, two segments
curling up to the pale sting, always in the Sand colours so all stack
together; Mimic Glass one curved shard, a pale gleam and a smear of
amber oil; the Gloam Wisp a tuft of shadow shaped like a small flame
with a pale grey core. Otherwise each goes the Rat Tail's way (D-474):
the same seeded 1-in-2 coin, stacks of 20, and lines drafted for the
playtest.

**D-476 · 2026-10-30 · The rare drops** (the owner's picks, all
recommended; step 3 of D-473, narrows Q-061) Each is look A of the rare
drops mock-up: the Venom Sac a plump bulb of green venom with a pale
gleam and a short neck of Sand shell; the Mimic Heart a heart of amber
oil with a glass gleam; the Gloam Eye a round pale eye with the pink
iris, rimmed in shadow grey. The rare one rolls its own seeded coin,
about 1 in 10, apart from the common's 1 in 2, so both can fall: the
common where the creature fell, the rare on the nearest open tile.
Otherwise each goes the Rat Tail's way (D-474): stacks of 20, and lines
drafted for the playtest.

**D-477 · 2026-10-30 · The first creature recipe: a glass flask** (the
owner's picks, all recommended; step 4 of D-473, narrows Q-061) In the
bag, Make on Mimic Glass turns 1 Mimic Glass and 2 Oilcaps into an Oil
flask, a turn, as 3 Oilcaps still do (D-299); with too few of either the
button is dim. It makes a find we have, so nothing new is drawn. The
How to play Crafting page gets a row for it. Its lines are drafted for
the playtest.

**D-478 · 2026-10-30 · Torches made and set down** (the owner's picks,
all recommended; step 5 of D-473, builds D-459, D-455) In the bag, Make
on Moth Dust turns 1 Moth Dust and 1 Wood into a Torch, a turn; with
too few the button is dim. Torches stack 5, like flasks. Set down
from the bag the bench's way, but anywhere, in the depths too: a side
is picked, it goes on plain floor off the edge, never cutting the room
in two, a turn, and it blocks the way. It stands as look A of the step
5 mock-up, a stick in a ring of stones, its head wrapped in cloth, and
lights 3 tiles like a wall torch, so lampmoths come to it. It burns 100
turns by the clock, even while the hero is away, then goes out, leaving
bare floor; it can't be taken back. The How to play Crafting page gets
a row. Its lines are drafted for the playtest. Also moved D-474 to
D-476 to the archive, word for word, as this file neared 12 KB.

**D-474 · 2026-10-30 · The Rat Tail** (the owner's picks, all
recommended; step 1 of D-473, narrows Q-061) On the floor and in the
bag it is look A: the rat's pink tail curled into a loop, with a grey
stub where it joined the rat. The 1-in-2 coin is seeded like a flee's,
from the seed, the turn and the tile the rat fell on, so nothing new is
saved and a replay drops the same. Stacks of 20, as other small finds
(D-333). Three lines when taken and three when chosen in the bag, each
in turn, and a "can't carry more" line, drafted for the playtest. Left on the floor it
stays until taken and never grows back.

**D-475 · 2026-10-30 · The other four common drops** (the owner's
picks, all recommended; step 2 of D-473, narrows Q-061) Each is look A
of the drops mock-up: Moth Dust a small heap of pale dust with amber
glints and two motes; the Stinger the scorpion's tail tip, two segments
curling up to the pale sting, always in the Sand colours so all stack
together; Mimic Glass one curved shard, a pale gleam and a smear of
amber oil; the Gloam Wisp a tuft of shadow shaped like a small flame
with a pale grey core. Otherwise each goes the Rat Tail's way (D-474):
the same seeded 1-in-2 coin, stacks of 20, and lines drafted for the
playtest.

**D-476 · 2026-10-30 · The rare drops** (the owner's picks, all
recommended; step 3 of D-473, narrows Q-061) Each is look A of the rare
drops mock-up: the Venom Sac a plump bulb of green venom with a pale
gleam and a short neck of Sand shell; the Mimic Heart a heart of amber
oil with a glass gleam; the Gloam Eye a round pale eye with the pink
iris, rimmed in shadow grey. The rare one rolls its own seeded coin,
about 1 in 10, apart from the common's 1 in 2, so both can fall: the
common where the creature fell, the rare on the nearest open tile.
Otherwise each goes the Rat Tail's way (D-474): stacks of 20, and lines
drafted for the playtest.

**D-477 · 2026-10-30 · The first creature recipe: a glass flask** (the
owner's picks, all recommended; step 4 of D-473, narrows Q-061) In the
bag, Make on Mimic Glass turns 1 Mimic Glass and 2 Oilcaps into an Oil
flask, a turn, as 3 Oilcaps still do (D-299); with too few of either the
button is dim. It makes a find we have, so nothing new is drawn. The
How to play Crafting page gets a row for it. Its lines are drafted for
the playtest.

**D-478 · 2026-10-30 · Torches made and set down** (the owner's picks,
all recommended; step 5 of D-473, builds D-459, D-455) In the bag, Make
on Moth Dust turns 1 Moth Dust and 1 Wood into a Torch, a turn; with
too few the button is dim. Torches stack 5, like flasks. Set down
from the bag the bench's way, but anywhere, in the depths too: a side
is picked, it goes on plain floor off the edge, never cutting the room
in two, a turn, and it blocks the way. It stands as look A of the step
5 mock-up, a stick in a ring of stones, its head wrapped in cloth, and
lights 3 tiles like a wall torch, so lampmoths come to it. It burns 100
turns by the clock, even while the hero is away, then goes out, leaving
bare floor; it can't be taken back. The How to play Crafting page gets
a row. Its lines are drafted for the playtest. Also moved D-474 to
D-476 to the archive, word for word, as this file neared 12 KB.

**D-479 · 2026-10-30 · The spear, a second weapon** (the owner's picks,
all recommended; step 6 of D-473, builds D-457, D-252) The second kind
is a spear that hits through: walking into a creature hits it for 2,
as the knife does, and the creature awake behind it, in the line from
the hero, for 1; one asleep in disguise there is left as it is. The
front one's line is said. It lies in depth 3's first room, the one with
the stairs up, by the knife's rule, and not while the hero has one.
Found with a weapon held, a weapon goes into the bag, 1 a slot; with
the bag full it stays on the floor, "Your bag is full." Hold on it in
the bag swaps it with the one in hand, a turn, the bag staying open.
It looks as the step 6 mock-up drew it, a wooden shaft with a steel
head. Its lines are drafted for the playtest.

**D-480 · 2026-10-30 · The Stone Pick and copper** (the owner's picks,
all recommended; step 7 of D-473, builds D-450, D-455, D-451) The first
pick is the Stone Pick, a second slot at the workbench: 2 Wood and 3
Stone, dim with too few, one carried. It is never held; with it in the
bag, a Copper vein breaks. From depth 3, one of the 3 deposits in each
Cave room is a vein; without the pick a bump says it is too hard,
free. 4 bumps give 1 Copper Ore, stacks of 20, for crafts later; a
vein grows back after 1800 turns. Look A of each in the step 7
mock-up: a one-point stone head on a wooden handle, the deposit's
boulder flecked with copper, a grey lump flecked with copper. The How
to play Crafting page gets a row. Its lines are drafted for the
playtest.

**D-481 · 2026-10-30 · The Growth tree** (the owner's picks; step 8 of
D-473, builds D-462, D-293) Growth is drawn as look D of the step 8
mock-up, the owner's mix of three columns and a drawn tree: an ember
glows at the root, the trunk splits into three limbs, Light, Body and
Hands, and each climbs through its branch's places, the first row at
the bottom. Today's hearts are Body's first upgrade, with a row of
hearts bought. Light and Hands show dim dashed places "to come"; one
more row shows dim and locked, "opens deeper". The arrows or a tap pick
a place, outlined gold; right of a line it says the embers left, its
name and words, and its button with the cost. Upgrades are free to buy
in any open row, in any order: nothing bought closes or opens another.
It still opens as the Journal's Growth tab. Also moved D-477 and D-478
to the archive, word for word, as this file passed 12 KB.

**D-483 · 2026-10-30 · The Copper Wick** (the owner's picks, all
recommended; step 9 of D-482, builds D-459) The first lantern part is
the Copper Wick, in the lantern's wick slot. It is made at the
workbench, its third slot, from 3 Copper Ore and 1 Wood, and only one
is carried or fitted. Fitted, it burns oil a quarter slower: a full
tank lasts 75 turns, not 60; oil alone still sets the reach (D-014).
Choosing it in the bag shows Fit, which puts it in the lantern, free,
with "You fit the copper wick." Swapping and taking parts out wait for
a second part; no mark on the lantern button and no lantern row in the
bag for now. Its bag picture is look A of the mock-up: an upright cloth
wick with a burnt tip in a wide copper collar.

**D-484 · 2026-10-30 · The Rat Hide Vest** (the owner's picks, all
recommended; step 10 of D-482, builds D-458) Armour comes first in step
10, the two charms after, each its own step. The first armour piece is
the Rat Hide Vest, leatherwork, made at the workbench, its fourth slot,
from 4 Rat Tail and 1 Wood (Rat Tail's first use); one is carried or
worn. Choosing it in the bag shows Wear, which puts it on, free, "You
put on the rat hide vest." Worn, each bite has a 1 in 4 chance, from
the seed, to glance off and hurt nothing; when no bite that turn hurts,
"The bite glances off your vest." A glancing bite is still a bite: its
sound plays and a walk stops. Taking it off, and a mark that it is worn,
wait for now. Its bag picture, a brown vest with grey fur at the
shoulders laced in pink, and its lines are drafted for the playtest.

**D-485 · 2026-10-30 · Room on the crafting pages** (the owner's picks,
all recommended; the owner's ask) The workbench card's grid is 4 × 3,

narrower. How to play's Crafting tab becomes two, Bag (Make, Oilcap,
Glass, Dust, Wood) and Bench (Bench, Make, Pick, Wick, Vest), so the
Copper Wick and the vest get rows. Keyboard keeps its full name: five
tabs fit, so the mock-up's "Keys" was not needed. Also moved D-481 to
the archive, word for word, as this file neared 12 KB.

**D-486 · 2026-10-30 · The Wisp Charm** (the owner's picks, all
recommended; step 10 of D-482, builds D-458) The first charm is the
Wisp Charm, named for its material, made at the workbench, its fifth
slot, from 3 Gloam Wisp and 1 Copper Ore (Gloam Wisp's first use); one
is carried or worn. Wear, in the bag, puts it on, free, in a charm
slot, "You put on the wisp charm." Worn, a beaten creature leaves its
rare material about 1 in 5, not 1 in 10; the common stays 1 in 2. Its
bag picture, a gloam wisp in a round copper setting on a small loop,
and its lines are drafted for the playtest. Also moved D-483 to the
archive, word for word, as this file neared 12 KB.

**D-513 · 2026-10-31 · Change look, and taps big enough** (the owner's
picks, both recommended; builds D-289) The slot card's look button
reads CHANGE LOOK, as a wide button of its own over RENAME at the left
and DELETE at the right. The buttons look the same size, but each
row's taps reach past them, at least 44 px tall on a phone.

**D-514 · 2026-10-31 · More looks, the plan** (the owner's picks;
builds D-280, D-418) Cloak patterns come first; then more clothes
shapes and more colours. New look parts later, each its own step: a
hood or hat, the lantern's style, a scarf or cape. A few new looks are
free in the picker; the special ones, like patterns, wait for the look
currency (Q-055). Change look grows into one card with a tab per part
and the hero shown big. Which patterns, and which are free, is Q-073.

**D-515 · 2026-10-31 · The first cloak patterns** (the owner's picks,
all recommended, from a mock-up; answers Q-073, builds D-514) Zebra
stripes, cow spots and camo, the three D-280 named. Each has its own
colours, not the cloak's: zebra bone with dark stripes, cow bone with
dark patches, camo moss with brown and deep green blotches. None is
free; all three wait for the look currency (Q-055).

**D-516 · 2026-10-31 · Glints, the look currency** (the owner's picks,
both recommended; narrows Q-055, builds D-418, D-467) The look currency
is called Glints. The Feats tiers pay them, and they are spent in the
Look card: a locked look shows there with a lock and is bought there.
Which Feats pay, how many Glints, and each look's price stay Q-055.

**D-520 · 2026-10-31 · The hero creator, step 1: the big hero** (the
owner's picks, both recommended; builds D-517) The Cloak card comes
first: the hero 3× on a lit floor, the profile's name over it and the
cloak's name under it, sideways at the left of the boxes. The six boxes
stay three to a row, shorter: each hero 1× with its name. Eyes, trim and
clothes join the card as tabs in the next steps.
*Partly superseded by D-523 (2026-10-31): the hero as big as fits.*
*Partly superseded by D-526 (2026-10-31): the boxes' heroes 2× when they fit.*

**D-521 · 2026-10-31 · The hero creator, step 2: the tabs** (the
owner's picks, all recommended; builds D-517, D-520) One LOOK card:
four tabs, CLOAK, EYES, TRIM and CLOTHES, in a row under its heading,
the open one in ember. Every tab's boxes show the whole hero 1× in that
choice, named; the big hero shows the details. OK finishes from any
tab. The arrows go up onto the tabs, the flame on a tab opens it, and
left and right walk them.

**D-522 · 2026-10-31 · The hero creator, step 3: RANDOM** (the owner's
picks, all recommended; builds D-517, D-521) RANDOM is a button at the
left of OK on the LOOK card, on a new hero's and on Change look. One
press rolls all four parts at once from the free looks (every look is
free for now), and the flame stays on RANDOM to roll again; the open
tab stays open. With the arrows, RANDOM sits under the first two
columns and OK under the third.
*Partly superseded by D-523 (2026-10-31): a row of big buttons.*

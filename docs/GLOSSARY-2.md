## Creatures
- **Rat:** chases once it notices the hero (D-126, D-226), and once hurt flees for 4 turns or stands its ground, on a coin toss (D-292, D-301). From depth 3, about 1 room in 3, the rat by the flask comes with 2 packmates: once one notices, all do, each heading for its own free side of the hero; with 2 or more of its pack left, a hurt rat stands its ground (D-727, D-728).
- **Gloamling:** chases, and shuns light (D-240); by night in one woods room round home (D-780).
- **Lampmoth:** a pale moth that seeks light, in the pool from depth 2 and below (D-356, D-534); by night in each woods room round home (D-778).
- **Flask mimic:** looks just like an oil flask until bumped; then it wakes, with eyes and teeth, and chases for 6 turns before it settles back down; about 1 room in 4 on depths 3 to 5 (D-366, D-368).
- **Scorpion:** drawn from above; one hit kills it; in the pool from depth 3 and below (D-370, D-534). Its way is a tail strike: it comes as one that chases does but never bites beside the hero; in line within 2 tiles it raises its tail a turn, then strikes both tiles ahead for 2, then rests a turn (D-729).
- **Cave spider:** drawn from above, low and squat; shuns light only, so it waits in the dark and bites only from there; from depth 2 (D-535).
- **Bat:** drawn from above, wings spread; seeks light, and once hurt flees for 4 turns or stands, on a coin toss; from depth 3 (D-537); by night as the lampmoth (D-779).
- **Mole:** drawn side on, as the rat is; never flees, 4 hearts; from depth 5 (D-541). It burrows: it notices the hero only within 5 steps, by day or night (D-858), then digs down and comes under the floor as a mound that can't be hit, up beside the hero, biting the turn after; once up, or stuck with no tile toward the hero free, it chases on foot (D-726, D-857); by day in each woods room round home (D-781).
- **Snapcap:** a heartcap that bites; never moves, looks just like a heartcap until the hero stands beside it, then shows eyes and teeth; 2 hearts; from depth 2 (D-543).
- **Herb mimic:** looks just like a herb until bumped; then it wakes, with eyes and teeth, and chases for 6 turns before it settles back down; 2 hearts; from depth 3 (D-549); by day in one woods room round home (D-793).
- **Bone rat:** a rat with nothing left but bone; chases and never flees, 3 hearts; from depth 4 (D-549, D-551). Beaten, it falls into a **pile of bones** that stands up again 3 turns later; stepping onto it **scatters** it for good (D-731).
- **Warden:** an old stone guard made by Underhearth's people to guard their things; asleep as a statue until bumped or its locket taken, then chases; 4 hearts; beside the locket (D-692).
- **Oil tick:** a round tick swollen with amber oil; chases, and beside the hero drinks 5% of the lantern's oil instead of biting, biting only once it is empty; 2 hearts; from depth 3 (D-606). The wicksucker of the list, named the oil tick for now.
- **Lure wisp:** a small cold flame with two pink eyes; when the hero comes within 3 steps it drifts a tile away, toward the nearest other creature; it never bites, and its glow shows in the dark though it lights no tiles; 1 heart; from depth 4 (D-649).
- **Bunny:** drawn side on, soft brown with a pale tail and a dark eye; the first creature that means no harm: it never bites, and hops away once the hero is within 3 steps; 1 heart; by day only, in the home room and each woods room round home (D-784).
- **Skulker crab:** a crab under a stony grey shell, rust legs and claws; never moves, looks like a loose stone until the hero is beside it, then pinches; 2 hearts; by day only, in each mountain room (D-796).
- **Rim lurker:** a hunched shade of dusky violet, long arms, pale claws; lurks: waits by the mountain rock till the hero comes within 3 steps, lit or not, then chases; 3 hearts, claws for 2; by night only, in each mountain room (D-797).
- **Centipede:** drawn side on, rust red with pale legs and green fangs; chases, and its bite leaves the hero poisoned; 2 hearts; from depth 3 (D-654).
- **Spit grub:** a glowworm drawn side on, a plump pale grub with a glowing green tail; never moves, and its glow shows in the dark; spits a glob at the lit lantern down a clear row or column, then rests; 1 heart; from depth 4 (D-659, D-688).
- **Glob:** the Spit grub's spit, a glowing green ball flying a tile a turn; it hits the hero or a creature for 1, and breaks on walls and rubble (D-659, D-688).
- **Poisoned:** the first debuff, from Venom (D-636): 6 turns, a heart every 3, 2 in all, the last heart too since the Antidote (D-655); a new bite starts it again; a green drop mark on red glass beside the boon's, its time left on its rim (D-652, D-654, D-848).
- **Stiff:** waking from sleeping out (D-758): 20 turns, 1 swing in 3 misses; a pale blue Zz mark on red glass beside poisoned's, its time left on its rim (D-848); a fall ends it (D-759).
- **Warmed:** sitting by a burning campfire (D-845): blazing 60 turns, embers 30, the lantern burning slower, a full tank lasting 70, added to Slowburn; a small orange flame mark, its time left on its rim, embers starting half lit (D-847); a fall ends it.
- **Colour set:** one of the colours a creature's single drawing may wear, picked by the seed: the scorpion's Sand, Rust, Coal, Olive and Bone (D-373).
- **Chases, Shuns light, Seeks light:** the ways creatures have so far (D-269, D-351); **Flees when hurt**, 1 in 2, for 4 turns (D-292, D-301); **drinks**, oil instead of a bite, the first light way (D-606); **Lures**, drifting away toward another creature (D-649); **Spits** (D-688); **Burrows**, digging down and coming under the floor, the mole's (D-726); **Pack**, rats that notice together and surround (D-727, D-728); **Tail strike**, the scorpion's (D-729); **Shy**, hopping just away, the bunny's (D-784); **Lurks**, waiting till the hero is within 3 steps, the rim lurker's (D-797).
- **Next move:** what a seen creature does if the hero waits (D-349).
- **Creature list:** the column of plaques under the glass panel, one for each
  creature that has noticed the hero; a plaque shows its face and next move (D-357, D-380).
  Tapping one **frames** its creature: amber corners, name and hearts (D-359).

## Rooms below
- **Hall, large room:** a generated room of 2 cells side by side or one above the other, or of 2 × 2 cells; side rooms, off the way to the stairs, with twice or four times a one-cell room's finds and creatures (D-645, D-648, D-651). A cell here is a block of a room's tiles, not a region's grid slot.
- **Dead end, cache:** the last room of a side branch, with one opening; it holds a cache, 2 or 3 extra finds (flask, herb, oilcap, heartcap) in its far corner, left once (D-650).

# Glossary, part 2

`GLOSSARY.md` continued, under its rules: use these words in the game,
the docs and the code; where this page and a decision disagree, the
decision wins. Creatures and people moved here word for word at 0.3.3
(D-531), as that page neared 8 KB; the home pieces at 0.3.27 (D-565).

## Creatures
- **Rat:** chases once it notices the hero (D-126, D-226), and once hurt flees for 4 turns or stands its ground, on a coin toss (D-292, D-301).
- **Gloamling:** chases, and shuns light (D-240).
- **Lampmoth:** a pale moth that seeks light, one room a floor on depths 2 to 4 (D-356).
- **Flask mimic:** looks just like an oil flask until bumped; then it wakes, with eyes and teeth, and chases for 6 turns before it settles back down; about 1 room in 4 on depths 3 to 5 (D-366, D-368).
- **Scorpion:** drawn from above; chases and stings for 2, but one hit kills it; one room a floor on depths 3 and 4 (D-370).
- **Cave spider:** drawn from above, low and squat; shuns light only, so it waits in the dark and bites only from there; from depth 2 (D-535).
- **Bat:** drawn from above, wings spread; seeks light, and once hurt flees for 4 turns or stands, on a coin toss; from depth 3 (D-537).
- **Mole:** drawn side on, as the rat is; chases and never flees, 4 hearts; from depth 5 (D-541).
- **Snapcap:** a heartcap that bites; never moves, looks just like a heartcap until the hero stands beside it, then shows eyes and teeth; 2 hearts; from depth 2 (D-543).
- **Herb mimic:** looks just like a herb until bumped; then it wakes, with eyes and teeth, and chases for 6 turns before it settles back down; 2 hearts; from depth 3 (D-549).
- **Bone rat:** a rat with nothing left but bone; chases and never flees, 3 hearts; from depth 4 (D-549, D-551).
- **Warden:** an old stone guard made by Underhearth's people to guard their things; asleep as a statue until bumped, then chases; 4 hearts; beside the locket (D-692).
- **Oil tick:** a round tick swollen with amber oil; chases, and beside the hero drinks 5% of the lantern's oil instead of biting, biting only once it is empty; 2 hearts; from depth 3 (D-606). The wicksucker of the list, named the oil tick for now.
- **Lure wisp:** a small cold flame with two pink eyes; when the hero comes within 3 steps it drifts a tile away, toward the nearest other creature; it never bites, and its glow shows in the dark though it lights no tiles; 1 heart; from depth 4 (D-649).
- **Centipede:** drawn side on, rust red with pale legs and green fangs; chases, and its bite leaves the hero poisoned; 2 hearts; from depth 3 (D-654).
- **Spit grub:** a glowworm drawn side on, a plump pale grub with a glowing green tail; never moves, and its glow shows in the dark; spits a glob at the lit lantern down a clear row or column, then rests; 1 heart; from depth 4 (D-659, D-688).
- **Glob:** the Spit grub's spit, a glowing green ball flying a tile a turn; it hits the hero or a creature for 1, and breaks on walls and rubble (D-659, D-688).
- **Poisoned:** the first debuff, from Venom (D-636): 6 turns, a heart every 3, 2 in all, the last heart too since the Antidote (D-655); a new bite starts it again; a green drop mark beside the boon's (D-652, D-654).
- **Colour set:** one of the colours a creature's single drawing may wear, picked by the seed: the scorpion's Sand, Rust, Coal, Olive and Bone (D-373).
- **Chases, Shuns light, Seeks light:** the ways creatures have so far (D-269, D-351); **drinks**, oil instead of a bite, the first light way (D-606); **Lures**, drifting away toward another creature (D-649); **Spits** (D-688).
- **Next move:** what a seen creature does if the hero waits (D-349).
- **Creature list:** the column of plaques under the glass panel, one for each
  creature that has noticed the hero; a plaque shows its face and next move (D-357, D-380).
  Tapping one **frames** its creature: amber corners, name and hearts (D-359).

## People
- **Peddler:** the first person, found trapped in a ring of rubble on
  depth 3; freed by digging one heap, he stands at home in the woods,
  and is the first vendor: bumped there, his card opens and he buys
  creature materials and sells a flask and a torch (D-471, D-490,
  D-491, D-492, D-494, D-495).
- **Trade coins (coins):** earned only by selling to a vendor, each find
  at its own price: 1 for most common creature materials (Wisp Glass 2),
  5 for a rare one; kept on a fall, but not
  when Hardcore starts over; spent at a vendor (D-472, D-494, D-495,
  D-649, D-674).
- **Rubble:** a heap of fallen rock, solid, light passing over it; dug
  away by hand in 3 bumps (D-490, D-491).

## Home
- **Workbench:** made in the bag from 3 Wood, set down on the surface;
  bigger things are made at it, on its panel, from the bag and chests near
  (D-560), where Deconstruct workbench puts it back in the bag (D-314,
  D-319, D-320, D-322, D-323).
- **Stash chest:** made at the workbench from 3 Wood and 3 Stone, set down
  on the surface, opened by walking into it; each one keeps 12 slots of its
  own, a picked stack moving between it and the bag, and taken down only
  when empty (D-021, D-324, D-325, D-326, D-330, D-331, D-340).
- **Bed, sleep, rested:** the first home piece, from the workbench, set
  down on the surface; bumped from dusk, the hero sleeps until morning
  and wakes healed and rested, a gold heart after the red (D-488).
- **Garden bed:** made at the workbench from 3 Wood and 2 Stone, set
  down on the surface; a box of soil where a seed is planted, one plant
  each; bumped, its card, Plant or Deconstruct (D-562, D-565, D-566).
- **Shelf:** made at the workbench from 4 Wood and 2 Stone, set down on
  the surface; it holds the relics found, empty for now; bumped, it asks
  Deconstruct shelf? (D-689, D-690).
- **Plant, sprout:** Plant on the garden bed's card puts a Gloamberry
  Seed in it; a planted bed shows a sprout until it grows (D-566).
- **Grow:** a sprout grows into a small gloamberry bush 300 turns after
  planting, every turn counting (D-567).
- **Feed:** Feed on a sprout's card puts 1 Bat Guano on its soil, and it
  grows in 150 turns from planting instead of 300, once (D-569).
- **Station:** a place to make one bigger craft family, built at home on
  the surface: forge, kitchen fire, power bench, altar (D-455). Not in
  the game yet.
- **Waystone:** a 3 × 3 standing-stone circle: a walkable cross, an old
  mossy stone in each corner tile, the plate in the middle (D-579, D-587,
  D-620). The home
  waystone stands in the woods, asleep until the hero steps on its
  plate; woken, its rune is lit violet (D-589, D-591). Others sleep at the
  top of each band of floors, depths 2, 5 and 8, then every 4th depth,
  12, 16, 20… (D-579, D-592, D-594). On a woken plate, the travel card goes to another (D-593).
- **Boon:** a small help picked, 1 of 3, at the home waystone as the
  hero sets out; it lasts until home again or a fall (D-583). Stepping
  onto the woken home plate offers 3, each of a different kind; the list
  is `src/core/boons.js` (D-596).
- **Mark:** a small glass square under the hearts panel with a picture:
  the boon held, its kind's from its card; tapped, it says what it is
  (D-604, `src/render/marks.js`).

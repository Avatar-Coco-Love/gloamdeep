# Changelog

Newest first. Every PR adds exactly one entry, and that entry is the
version bump. The same lines go, word for word, into the in-game Version
history, `src/core/history-0.3.js` (D-254, D-528).

Versions:
- `0.0.x`: pre-production (decisions, docs, art studies). No playable game.
- `0.1.x`: foundation. The first code, built up one small visible step at a time.
- `0.2.x`: stage 0.2, from step 32: places with character, a dive worth
  repeating, and stronger, slowly (D-159).
- `0.3.x`: stage 0.3, the world (D-168), from 0.3.0, once stage 0.2's
  last steps were done (D-528).
- `0.4.0`: when the way to depth 9 opens, chapter 1 done (D-954).
- Later minor versions (`0.5`…) are set when the build plan in
  `docs/PROGRESS.md` defines the next stage.
- `1.0.0`: the first release the owner calls finished.

Entry format:

```
## 0.0.2 (2026-10-18)
- One line per change, written so the owner can follow it.
- Decisions: D-008. Questions closed: Q-001.
```

## 0.3.462 (2026-11-28)
- A lighter restart, nothing in the game changed. The next step is the
  hero at the Lampeater's door. Claude's earlier drafts are approved as
  they stand, and from now on Claude picks the small things (words,
  colours, looks, timings) and asks only about gameplay and story.
- Decisions: D-1026. D-1019 and D-1023 moved to the archive, word for word.

## 0.3.461 (2026-11-22)
- The spark speaks in light. Near a gloamling or the Lampeater, seen or
  not, its glow cools to violet and dims, and it warms again as they go.
  Deeper down, nearer the First Light, its glow is a little wider and
  brighter.
- Decisions: D-1025. D-1022 moved to the archive, word for word.

## 0.3.460 (2026-11-22)
- The story begins: the spark, the guide, waits on depth 2 in the first
  cave room, caught in a knot of gloam, dark threads with violet edges
  wound round a dim light. Bump the knot with the lantern lit and it melts:
  "The gloam lets go. A small warm light comes to your side." With the
  lantern off it holds: "The threads hold fast in the dark."
- Freed, the spark floats by the hero's shoulder everywhere, a small warm
  glow that lights nothing; the lantern stays the only light.
- A journey already past depth 2 finds the knot waiting in that room.
- Dev tools: a Spark switch in Hero frees the spark or takes it back.
- Decisions: D-1024. D-1021 moved to the archive, word for word.

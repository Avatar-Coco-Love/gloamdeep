# Changelog

Newest first. Every PR adds exactly one entry, and that entry is the
version bump. The same lines go, word for word, into the in-game Version
history, `src/core/history-0.3.js` (D-254, D-528).

Versions:
- `0.0.x`: pre-production (decisions, docs, art studies). No playable game.
- `0.1.x`: foundation. The first code, built up one small visible step at a time.
- `0.2.x`: stage 0.2, from step 32: places with character, a dive worth
  repeating, and stronger, slowly (D-159).
- `0.3.x`: stage 0.3, the world (D-168), from 0.3.0, once stage 0.2's
  last steps were done (D-528).
- `0.4.0`: when the way to depth 9 opens, chapter 1 done (D-954).
- Later minor versions (`0.5`…) are set when the build plan in
  `docs/PROGRESS.md` defines the next stage.
- `1.0.0`: the first release the owner calls finished.

Entry format:

```
## 0.0.2 (2026-10-18)
- One line per change, written so the owner can follow it.
- Decisions: D-008. Questions closed: Q-001.
```

## 0.3.444 (2026-11-20)
- Herbs and mushrooms are rooted now, as the flowers are: the healing herb,
  the oilcap and the heartcap stand on their own tile, still, their foot in
  a tuft of earth, no longer lifted and bobbing over a shadow. A herb has a
  grass blade either side; a mushroom stands in earth alone.
- A herb mimic asleep and a snapcap shut stand rooted too, just like the
  finds they copy. Other finds still bob.
- Decisions: D-1008.

## 0.3.443 (2026-11-20)
- New: the Glowcup, the second flower, a deep teal cup round a pale green
  heart on a stem with two long leaves. One grows in about half the rooms
  of every floor from depth 1 down, never in a vault, the arena or its
  hallway, and never behind a Gap. It only looks bright: it gives no light.
- Walk onto one to put it in the bag, in stacks of 20, for crafts later.
  Picked, it comes back a day later on another floor tile of the room, as
  the duskstar does. Each has its own slight shade and faces its own way.
- Flowers are rooted now: the duskstar and the glowcup stand on their own
  tile, still, their stem in a tuft of earth with a grass blade either
  side, no longer lifted and bobbing over a shadow. Other finds still bob.
- Decisions: D-1007.

## 0.3.442 (2026-11-20)
- New: the lantern's three slots, Wick, Tank and Glass, show in the bag,
  a LANTERN row beside the gear row on a phone held sideways, under it on
  one held upright, the bag's slots a row lower there. A fitted part shows
  in its slot; an empty one shows its kind faintly, the glass a plain pane,
  as no glass is found yet.
- Tap a lantern slot, or reach it with the arrows, to see the part's name,
  its slot, its numbers and its Look line, as for a gear slot. A part still
  goes in with Fit, swapping the one there; nothing comes off.
- Decisions: D-1006.

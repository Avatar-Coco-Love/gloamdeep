# Changelog

Newest first. Every PR adds exactly one entry, and that entry is the
version bump. The same lines go, word for word, into the in-game Version
history, `src/core/history-0.3.js` (D-254, D-528).

Versions:
- `0.0.x`: pre-production (decisions, docs, art studies). No playable game.
- `0.1.x`: foundation. The first code, built up one small visible step at a time.
- `0.2.x`: stage 0.2, from step 32: places with character, a dive worth
  repeating, and stronger, slowly (D-159).
- `0.3.x`: stage 0.3, the world (D-168), from 0.3.0, once stage 0.2's
  last steps were done (D-528).
- `0.4.0`: when the way to depth 9 opens, chapter 1 done (D-954).
- Later minor versions (`0.5`…) are set when the build plan in
  `docs/PROGRESS.md` defines the next stage.
- `1.0.0`: the first release the owner calls finished.

Entry format:

```
## 0.0.2 (2026-10-18)
- One line per change, written so the owner can follow it.
- Decisions: D-008. Questions closed: Q-001.
```

## 0.3.458 (2026-11-21)
- A trip by waystone now has its own moment, about 2 seconds. The hero
  breaks into violet sparks that drift up like embers, the room fades, the
  far stone stands empty with its plate glowing, then the sparks drift down
  and settle back into the hero. A tap or a key skips it. With reduced
  motion it is a quick fade instead.
- Decisions: D-1022. D-1017 moved to the archive, word for word.

## 0.3.457 (2026-11-21)
- Each waystone now looks like the place it stands in. Home keeps its
  mossy stones in the grass. In the cave (depth 2) they are rough grey
  boulders with pebbles at their feet; in the warren (depth 5) brown
  stones wrapped in pale roots, with dirt heaped round them; in the
  dungeon (depth 8 and down) tall cut obelisks on a plinth, with neat
  square flagstones. The plate's violet rune is the same everywhere.
- Decisions: D-1021. D-1016 moved to the archive, word for word.

## 0.3.456 (2026-11-21)
- Docs: ideas from "how will it be special?" are kept for later in a
  new page, docs/RESEARCH-3.md: light decides the boss fights, your
  lights a map of your journey, the surface answers the deep, other
  players' light, light-routing puzzles, and the bait as the player's own
  pull. Nothing in the game changes.
- Decisions: D-1020.
